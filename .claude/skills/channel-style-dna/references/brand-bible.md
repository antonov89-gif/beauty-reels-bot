# Brand bible and locks

## Tiered style, not a single adjective
1. **Brand core, stable:** logo + usage limits; recurring palette; type hierarchy; intended audience/voice; unmistakable shapes/motifs; 'never do' constraints.
2. **Channel cinematic DNA, conditional:** photoreal/animated stylization, naturalness, lens/shot family, light & color, motion/transition language, 3D/2.5D, sound/VO. Require evidence from own work or brand approval.
3. **Series format:** explainer, vlog, cinematic short, interview, thriller, comedy, etc., each allowed its own genre camera/lens/speed while preserving brand identity.
4. **Episode-specific:** characters, locations, costumes, props, emotionally motivated shifts; never locked across unrelated series unless explicitly recurring.

## Guard against bland uniformity
- Do not paint every scene in channel logo colors. Keep cinematic skin tones, day/night physics and motivated lights. Accent palette primarily via set design, wardrobe, UI, grading boundaries, graphical highlights and thumbnails.
- Avoid repeated 'logo stamped on every frame' or forced camera style. Identity should persist in direction, framing choices, voice, graphic treatment, intentional recurring cues.
- Preserve contrast: different scenes can be bright/dark/intimate/epic while recognizably from the same studio.
- Maintain a *range* with allowed deviations rather than one immutable shot recipe. Record both `must_keep` and `can_vary`.

## Brand approval
`DRAFT` → `PROPOSED` → `APPROVED`, only after user's explicit approval. Approved version saved with hash/version, date and summary. New proposed revision coexists with old approved version until approved; rejected draft doesn't displace it. Approval of **look/identity** is not authorization for spending or publishing.

## Minimal brand lock checklist
- 3–5 recognizable channel traits actually supported or intentionally approved.
- logo/graphic and text consistency where shown;
- visual realism/animation treatment with evidence level;
- camera/lighting language and permitted range;
- voice/sound personality (if evidence or explicit approval);
- allowed variations for genre and competitors;
- forbidden mimicry, packaging, aesthetic mismatch.
