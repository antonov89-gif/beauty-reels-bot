# Brand-native cinematic production + QC

## Sequence design
Brand DNA first; original story causal beat second; competitor mechanic third; camera shot choices fourth. For a pilot, create 4–8 connected shots / 20–40 sec with proper axis/screen direction, geography, blocking, perspective, lens impression, motivated key practicals, environmental light, natural motion blur, human weight/physics and edit audio bridges. Mix wide/medium/close/insert according to narrative; no arbitrary camera orbit, endless zoom or forced palette everywhere. Frame numbers and technical parameters are approximate creative settings unless rendered/sourced.

## Channel style expression by department
- Direction: recurring original protagonist or visual motif when suitable; matching emotion intensity without borrowing story.
- Camera: channel-appropriate lens family, camera mass, believable tracking, explicit start/path/end and genre-dependent exceptions.
- Art/light: recognizable palette placed in props/environment/graphic overlays; practical light physics remain correct.
- Animation: weight, anticipation, realistic overlaps/secondary motion; consistent motion grammar; no canned perpetual push-in.
- Typography: consistent headline/subtitle treatment, safe zones, accessible mobile sizing; exact fonts only when known/licensed.
- Audio: approved language and performance, VO rhythm, Foley, ambience, diegetic perspective, mix; preserve brand's sonic motifs without cloning other voices or tracks.
- Packaging: own thumbnail signature + competitor's *information promise mechanics*, not competitor thumbnail template pixel-for-pixel.

## Acceptance gate
Review actual rendered media for:
**BRAND_FIT**: logo, typography/graphics, look, grading, approved voice/sound; '**HARD FAIL**' if plainly inconsistent with approved style or if competitor branding copied.
**FILMCRAFT**: camera geometry, performance, physics, motivated light, editing geography, dialogue/foley; hard fail for focal errors.
**ORIGINALITY**: original plot, shot composition and assets; hard fail for unlicensed duplication.
**MECHANIC_EFFECT**: clearly legible hook, tension / payoff and intended transferable technique; do not misstate predicted retention as observed analytics.
**CONTINUITY**: character, objects, season, state, physics and sound across cuts.
If a category isn't observable (no video/audio) mark `NOT_TESTED`, not 10/10.

Qualitative `PASS / REVISE / REJECT / NOT_TESTED` per category. Optional 0–10 *human rubric* only with actual artifact evidence and rationale; not a scientific match score. Repair order: edit/trimming → retime / grade / sound → local rework → regenerate focal shot → revise method. Inspect newly produced artifact before approval. No silent paid generation.
