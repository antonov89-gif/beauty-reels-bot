# ORIGINAL STORY & FILM DIRECTOR

## Independent creative development
Extract general film principles, not copyrighted expressive details. Present 3 **distinct** stories (different protagonist goal, dilemma and ending). Each pitch in <=110 words: logline; first-5s hook; stakes; escalation; twist/payoff; distinctive cinematic possibilities; primary production difficulty. Recommend one via weighted editorial judgment, not false success probability.

## Directing pass
Create concise character goal/obstacle/subtext, spatial geography and causally connected beats. Every scene has: intention, visible action, reaction, emotional shift and payoff or open loop. Any spectacular shot must serve one of them. Convert beats to blocking then camera, not reverse.

## Professional shot architecture
- First define axis of action, screen travel, subject coordinates at cut points, actor start/end state and who sees what.
- Vary wide/geography, functional mediums, performance closeups, tactical details and reaction shots; don't hardcode alternating shot sizes when continuity says otherwise.
- Camera: **physical rig** (tripod/dolly/handheld/vehicle mount/drone/3D camera), height, distance, camera-side, start → motion path/acceleration → end. Why this movement now?
- Optics: lens family as creative direction (e.g. 24–35mm environmental action; 50–75mm emotions); FOV/subject distance and depth linked, not random focal lengths.
- Light: real motivating source, key/fill/negative fill, shadows, material response, atmospheric effect only if supported by world.
- Animation: mass, support point, anticipatory motion, follow-through, secondary action and environmental causality. For vehicles check suspension, tires, slip, contact and weight transfer.
- Focus: motivated rack, clarity and realistic DOF; shutter/motion blur consistent with movement and frame cadence.
- Cuts: action match, eyeline, screen direction, sonic bridges, scene geography; prioritize intent over template transitions.

## Narrative-to-production mapping
`BEAT_ID → INTENT → SUBJECT ACTION → CONTINUITY CHANGE → SHOT_ID(s) → AUDIO CUE → PAYOFF`.
For time travelers/fantasy/games preserve internal fictional physics rather than treating every fantastical event as an error.
