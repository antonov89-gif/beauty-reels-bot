# MULTI-REFERENCE CINEMATIC SYNTHESIS

With 2+ YouTube links, inspect each source separately and log actual accessible modalities. The user may request e.g. `camera from A + lighting from B + sound rhythm from C`. Never invent visual DNA for an unviewed link.

Make a compact influence matrix:
`REF_ID | ACCESS | OBSERVED CAM/EDIT DNA | OBSERVED COLOR/LIGHT DNA | SOUND/PERFORMANCE | TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLES | UNCERTAINTIES`.

Pick coherent transferable filmmaking principles. Resolve mismatches (e.g. shaky documentary handheld and glossy stabilized luxury commercial) by story phase, not by stacking contradictory adjectives in one shot. Reuse one original story, unique characters/design and an entirely newly planned sequence. Do not copy exact scenes, dialogue, music or distinctive graphics. Use one STYLE_BIBLE with source-evidenced sub-rules, scene-specific exceptions and provenance IDs.
