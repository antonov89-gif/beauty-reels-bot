# PILOT FILM STUDIO — CONTINUOUS 20–40 SECONDS

## Upgrade from isolated 2–3 shots
Prove a **connected edited sequence**. Target 20–40 seconds, normally 4–8 shots; duration/count are adaptive to pacing. Must demonstrate camera continuity, blocking, lens/light consistency, physical action, natural dialogue/foley/ambience, dramatic arc and edit rhythm. Keep it a manageable high-risk mini-story, not an expensive showreel.

## Sequence design
- Opening: visually legible hook and situation.
- Orientation: geography and subject goal.
- Escalation: character/vehicle/prop interaction, one difficult technical case.
- Reaction: close-up or cut with continuity of emotion and voice.
- Payoff/turn: ends with a crisp meaningful beat and connectable final state.

## Example adaptable pilot table
| Shot | Time range | Purpose | Continuity + camera + sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | 0–4s | Unusual hook | Fast-read wide, stable geography, environmental bed |
| P02 | 4–9s | Protagonist intention | Motivated medium tracking, left-to-right action, sound bridge |
| P03 | 9–14s | Complication | Reverse with eye-line and axis logic, new offscreen cue |
| P04 | 14–20s | Physical proof | Close physical contact or vehicle movement, foley exact sync |
| P05 | 20–27s | Acting reaction | Performance close-up, consistent key light, voice/breath |
| P06 | 27–35s | Payoff | Wide new spatial understanding; sound/image resolve, end state |

These are **examples only**, not observations about an unseen reference; adapt tempo and shots to the proven reference DNA. Before generation define expected timeline and required assets. First request approval for any paid batches; without render tools create detailed pilot spec and call status `PILOT_SPEC` only.

## Accept or reject
Every shot passes individual QC **and** the edited 20–40s sequence passes: narrative comprehension, geography/axis, camera/subject/prop continuity, color matching, sound/voice perspective, emotional pacing, memorable hook/payoff. No per-shot approval implies automatic sequence pass. If 2 repair cycles don't clear severe errors, propose new technique rather than hiding defects.
