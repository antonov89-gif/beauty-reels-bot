## 2. Monetisation gap (YouTube Help, 27.10.2026)
Paths: 1,000 subscribers + 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days, OR 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public long-form watch hours in 12 months.
- Shorts path: need ~111k views/day; we do ~1.5k/day -> 74x gap. Linear growth cannot close it; it needs 3-5 hits of 1-3M within one 90-day window.
- Subscribers: 56/month -> 1,000 in ~17 months at the current rate; one 2M-view hit at our 1.22 subs/1k = ~2,400 subscribers.
- Conclusion: the plan must be built around producing hits (variance), not steady 1.2k uploads.

## 3. Professor's analysis: why we are capped
1. Topic pool too small: one exclave/town interests few people; hits are about a whole country, a continent, the planet, or "you" (deep analysis §3).
2. Hook fails half the viewers (49% engaged): frame 0 + first line do not create a question strong enough to stop the swipe.
3. Single-fact structure: no escalation, so no reason to watch to the end and share (shares 0.33/1k).
4. Middle length (35-45 s): neither a loop (6-12 s, rewatch) nor a story (50-90 s, escalation).
5. No identity: changing styles, two AI voices, no recurring series -> low subscribe reason.
6. Volume of tests without kill rules: many one-off topics, no repeated templates to learn from.
Strength to keep: clean licensing (Esri-free, own music), fact-checking, map engine, audience already in the US/IN/PH/GB = the same pool as the million-view geo channels.

## 4. Top-creator view (how a million-subscriber Shorts creator would run it)
1. One promise, one look: "The map shows what nobody told you" - same narrator voice, same colours, same opening sting, same end question.
2. Packaging first: write 10 hook lines per idea, keep the 1 that a stranger would stop for; frame 0 = the subject already highlighted + the claim.
3. Formats with proof only: STORY (walk/route, strangest borders of a famous country, why X% lives here) and LOOP (one map, one line).
4. Series and volume: 2 uploads/day, 3 episodes per template, kill a template after 3 misses, double down on any 3x outlier within 24 h (Part 2 next day).
5. Comment engine: end with a choice question, pin a debate comment, reply to every comment in the first hour.
6. Cross-post the same file to TikTok and Instagram Reels the same day; news-jack geography events within 48 h.
7. Do NOT chase meme audio, countryballs, footballers or fake claims: fast views, but they kill the YPP review.

## 6. Decision and 30-day plan
- Mr. Buck stays geography, upgraded to A: 1 STORY (50-90 s) + 1 LOOP (6-12 s) per day, one narrator voice, series: Walk It, True Size, Closer Than You Think, Strangest Borders of [famous country].
- Other channel: B (3D stories), 2-3 per week, AI disclosed.
- Week 1: build the asset kit (icons, counters, SFX, opening sting, end card), produce 6 STORY + 6 LOOP; facts from 2 sources each.
- Weekly review: swipe-away (target engaged ≥ 60%), likes ≥ 2%, comments ≥ 0.5/1k, subs ≥ 2/1k; any video at 3x median -> Part 2 within 24 h.
- Success gate at day 30: at least one video ≥ 100k, median ≥ 3k. If not met: switch the main series and re-test; if B outperforms A by 10x, move B to Mr. Buck after a YPP-risk review.
Honest outlook: hits are probabilistic; the sample shows tiny channels (0.6k-10k subs) reaching 1-10M with these formats, but no base rate is known, so no view number is promised.
