# Esri World Imagery: licence check for the channel (22.10.2026)

Question: may our Shorts use Esri World Imagery (satellite frames, virtual camera zooms) on a channel that wants to grow and may be monetised?
Method: Esri pages that could be opened or searched today. The binding documents (Esri Master Agreement E204, Product-Specific Terms of Use E300, the World Imagery item's terms) could NOT be opened in full: the Esri legal index is only a list of PDFs, the ArcGIS item page does not render in the fetch tool.

## What the sources say
| Source | Finding |
|---|---|
| Esri docs, "Static maps" (doc.arcgis.com, opened) | "Map image is the intellectual property of Esri and is used herein under license." Listed uses: personal, internal business and client presentations/reports, marketing of your own products on company sites, academic publications, government works. "For any other uses, you must first obtain permission from Esri." Examples of such uses: **commercial monetization and direct resale**. Attribution to Esri and its licensors is required on or near the map. **Videos are not addressed.** |
| Esri Canada guidance (search result) | Static maps/screenshots generally need no permission for academic or personal use or "most cases that don't involve resale or commercial use"; otherwise submit a copyright request to Esri. |
| Esri web terms (search result) | downloads are for internal/noncommercial use unless stated; Esri decides what counts as noncommercial or commercial; attribution required. |
| World Imagery item text (quoted on the OSM wiki) | limited grant to trace features and validate edits; every other use stays under the Master Agreement / Terms of Use. Maxar and other contributors keep their own rights. |
| E300 change logs (search) | basemap styles are for use only with Esri's platform basemap services (2023 footnote); ArcGIS Location Platform terms apply. Nothing on video. |
| Nexlev check (22.10) | the channel is NOT monetised at the moment. |

## Verdict
**NOT VERIFIED, with a real risk for the future.** Nothing found says that a public video is allowed; the only explicit rule lists "commercial monetization" as needing Esri's permission. As long as the channel is not monetised the risk is lower (attribution is shown on screen and in descriptions) but not zero: Esri decides what counts as commercial, we pull the imagery through the public export service (not via an Esri app), and the channel exists to grow.

## Actions
1. **Before the channel is monetised (YPP) or sponsored, get Esri's written permission** or stop using Esri imagery. Draft of the request below; only the user can send it.
2. Keep the on-screen and description attribution on every video (done everywhere).
3. New videos: prefer imagery with an open licence where the zoom level allows it:
   - globe, continents and regions: NASA Blue Marble / GIBS (public domain), already used;
   - regional views: Landsat (public domain) or Copernicus Sentinel-2 (Copernicus legal notice allows free use; text not opened today, verify before use). NOT the EOX "Sentinel-2 cloudless" layer: its current terms are CC BY-NC-SA, commercial use needs an EOX licence;
   - close zooms to rooftops: only Esri (or similar) imagery gives the resolution; national open orthophotos exist for some countries but their licence must be read first (Kartverket "Norge i bilder": access/licence not confirmed as open in the search).
   So for rooftop-level dives (Lofoten, Maldives) there is no confirmed free alternative today.
4. Ask the user whether to pause rooftop-level Esri zooms until the permission arrives (cost: such videos lose their close-up dive).

## Draft permission request (English, for the user to send via Esri's copyright/permissions contact)
Subject: Permission request: Esri World Imagery in short educational videos
Hello, I run the educational channel "Mr. Buck" (YouTube @Mr.Buck000, TikTok @mr.buck222) with short map-animation videos (15-90 seconds). We would like to use frames of Esri World Imagery (virtual camera zooms over satellite imagery) with the attribution "Imagery © Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics, and the GIS User Community" visible on screen and in every description. The channel may be monetised through platform ads in the future. Could you confirm in writing whether this use is permitted, and under which conditions or licence? Thank you.
