## 2. Two archetypes (not one)
**A. Narrated map story (#1, #2, #3, #7, #9): 52-103 s, 1.3M-61M.**
- Hook line states an extreme, concrete claim in ≤2 s and frame 0 already shows the subject highlighted (a photo on the map, a coloured country, a split, a pin on a border).
- The story is a chain of escalating obstacles or oddities; each beat gets a pictogram (skull, jail bars, frozen strait, passport stamp, "ACCIDENT" sign, "NO STAGE" stamp) and a number on screen (18 days, 80 km, -50°C, 2 km, 85 m, $170,000).
- Visual density is NOT extreme: 2.5-6 events / 10 s. Our E1 rule (≥10 events / 10 s) is above what the leaders do; the leaders win on meaning per beat, not on cut speed.
- 8-28 SFX tied to every pop/zoom; pulsing electronic bed; captions 1-4 words, bottom-centre, word-synced.
- Satellite base + saturated flat overlays (country fills, flags, glowing borders, dashed distance lines with counters).
- Ending: only #9 loops (zooms back to the opening map) and ends with a choice question ("Which door would you use?"); #1-#3 end on the destination.
- Likes 2.1-2.9% (2x ours) = high satisfaction; comments modest (0.13-0.54/1k).

**B. One-image loop (#5, #6, #10, #8): 6-23 s, 1.7M-30M.**
- One map or one 3D counter, one line of text, no narration; the joke or the number lands in 1-2 s; loops.
- Carried by meme audio (phonk, laugh samples) and meme visuals (countryhumans, trollface) -> high views, low likes (0.5-1.2%).
- For us: the format is usable only with own audio and no meme characters; expected ceiling lower than the originals (their sound is part of the hook). Treat as a cheap test, not the core.

**C. Controversy (#4): 7.3M, comments 1.55/1k (10x the others).**
- Driven by a claim that is very likely false: "the UN approved the Equal Earth map, only the US voted against" (no such UN vote is known to us; NOT VERIFIED, likely misinformation). Do NOT copy: misleading claims are a strike/YPP risk. The legit lesson: a true "the map you learned is wrong" angle produces debate.

## 4. Corrections to my earlier advice (27.10 docs)
1. "15-35 s only" was wrong for stories: the two biggest (61M, 47M) are 97-103 s. Length follows the number of escalating beats. Rule now: LOOP 6-12 s or STORY 50-90 s; avoid the 35-45 s middle unless the story is single-beat.
2. "≥10 visual events / 10 s" (E1) is not what the leaders do (2.5-6 / 10 s). Replace with: a new MEANING every 3-4 s (obstacle, number, icon, location).
3. "Find your country" works for loops; for stories the stronger ending is a choice/opinion question tied to the story (#9).
