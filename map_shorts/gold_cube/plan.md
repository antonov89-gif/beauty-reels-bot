Format 9:16, 1080×1920, ~32 s, English VO. Topic chosen for the channel (geography + money): all the gold ever mined.
Reference used only for its storytelling logic (thought experiment → physical demo → landmark comparison → bigger factor → human-scale answer → joke).
Own script, characters and compositions. **No paid generation has been started.**

---

## 1. Concept and hooks

**Concept.** Our miner character *Nugget* melts every ring, coin and bar on Earth into one cube. The cube looks huge in his hands, but next to the Eiffel Tower it is tiny. Then we add all the gold still in the ground (+30 % mass), and the cube grows by only ~2 m, because volume grows as the cube of the side. Finally the cube is split between 8.3 billion people. Each share is smaller than a sugar cube. Nugget looks at his crumb and pockets it.

**Hooks (first 3 s), pick one:**
1. *"What if we melted every bit of gold ever mined into one giant cube?"* — Nugget throws a gold ring into a glowing crucible. **(chosen)**
2. *"All the gold in human history fits in one cube. Guess how big."* — a gold cube drops from the sky onto an empty square.
3. *"Your share of all the gold on Earth is smaller than this."* — Nugget holds up a sugar cube.

## 2. Voice-over (91 words, Kokoro test read at 172 wpm ≈ 31.8 s)

> What if we melted every bit of gold ever mined into one giant cube?
> Rings, coins, bars from every vault. About two hundred twenty thousand tonnes.
> The cube? Just twenty-two and a half meters wide. It fits between the legs of the Eiffel Tower.
> Now add all the gold still buried underground. Thirty percent more gold, but the cube grows by only two meters.
> Split it between eight point three billion people, and your share is about thirty-five grams.
> A cube smaller than a sugar cube.
> Don't spend it all at once.

Delivery: male, mid-low register, conversational, light irony, close dry recording. Short pause (≈0.4 s) before "Just twenty-two and a half meters" and before "about thirty-five grams".

## 5. Prompts per scene (English, for an image-to-video or text-to-video model)

Each prompt = STYLE LOCK + the text below.

**S1 (3 s).** Start: Nugget stands next to a glowing crucible on a stand in a sunny workshop yard. Action: he flicks a small gold ring with his thumb; the ring spins through the air and drops into the crucible; molten gold splashes and glows brighter; Nugget grins at the camera. Camera: medium shot, slow steady push-in toward the crucible. End: close-up of the glowing molten surface. New info: we are melting gold.

**S2 (5 s).** Start: close-up of molten gold. Action: the camera pulls back to reveal a stylized Earth globe; golden streams of rings, coins and small bars rise from every continent and fly in arcs into a giant funnel above the crucible, melting into a bright river of gold. Camera: smooth pull-back to a full globe, then a gentle tilt up to the funnel. End: the funnel overflowing with a stream of molten gold. New info: all the world's gold.

**S3 (3.5 s).** Start: an enormous empty cube-shaped mould on a sunny city plaza, Nugget tiny at its base. Action: molten gold pours in, the level rises, the surface cools from orange to shiny polished gold, the mould walls fall outward with a heavy thud. Camera: low angle, slow rise to eye level, cube centred. End: a gleaming solid gold cube on the plaza. New info: the cube's size.

**S4 (3.5 s).** Start: the gold cube on the Champ de Mars lawn. Action: pigeons take off, Nugget shrugs. Camera: smooth crane pull-back and rise until the whole Eiffel Tower is visible, the cube sitting small between its four legs. End: wide shot, cube clearly much smaller than the tower's arch. New info: it is surprisingly small. Keep the Eiffel Tower's real proportions.

**S5 (4.5 s).** Start: ground in front of the cube. Action: the camera sinks into a clean cutaway cross-section of the ground showing rock layers with veins and nuggets of gold; a drill bores in, a conveyor lifts the nuggets to the surface and pours them onto the cube, whose surface ripples as it absorbs them. Camera: smooth descent into the cross-section, then rise alongside the conveyor. End: last load dumping onto the cube. New info: underground gold added.

**S6 (3.5 s).** Start: frontal view of the cube with a thin transparent ghost outline of its previous size. Action: the cube's edges push outward a little; Nugget looks up, confused, scratches his helmet. Camera: static with a slight push-in toward one edge. End: new cube only slightly bigger than the ghost. New info: 30% more gold, only ~2 m bigger.

**S7 (6 s).** Start: the full cube. Action: precise thin lines slice it into countless tiny cubes, which lift off as a glittering swarm and spread over a soft landscape filled with miniature people; one tiny cube floats down into Nugget's open palm, landing next to a white sugar cube that is clearly bigger. Camera: big smooth pull-back over the swarm, then a smooth glide down to a macro shot of the palm. End: macro of the gold crumb beside the sugar cube, held still. New info: about 35 g each.

**S8 (3 s).** Start: macro of the palm. Action: Nugget proudly pockets the gold crumb; a pigeon swoops in and snatches it from his pocket and flies off; Nugget's mustache droops. Camera: medium shot, gentle follow of the pigeon without whip pans. End: Nugget alone, deadpan. New info: comic ending.

## 6. NEGATIVE PROMPT and continuity rules

```
text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, subtitles, brand names, real currency logos,
distorted hands, extra fingers, extra limbs, melting faces, flickering, jittery or shaky camera,
random zoom pulses, chaotic rotation, morphing architecture, wrong Eiffel Tower shape,
photorealistic gore, dark or gloomy lighting, low contrast, blurry subject, cluttered background
```

Continuity rules:
- Nugget's design (hat, mustache, overalls, proportions) never changes. Use the same character reference frame for every shot.
- Lighting: one sun direction (upper left, ~45°) in all outdoor shots.
- The gold cube keeps the same material and colour. Its size changes only in S6 (+9 % edge).
- Scale is always honest: Nugget ≈ 1.6 m next to the 22.5 m cube (cube ≈ 14× his height); cube well below the tower's first platform (57 m).
- All numbers and captions are added in editing, never generated by the AI model.

## 7. Checks

**Duration.** 91 words. Test read with free Kokoro TTS: 31.9 s at speed 1.0 (162 wpm), **31.8 s at speed 1.1 (172 wpm)**, including 0.3 s gaps. The ElevenLabs voice at speed ≈1.05 should land at 31–32 s. Recheck after the final recording and re-time the table.

**Facts and sources.**
- Above-ground gold, end-2025: ≈219,900–220,700 t (two WGC pages differ) → we say "about 220,000 tonnes". WGC itself gives a cube of ≈22.5 m. [gold.org above-ground stocks](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/data/above-ground-stocks), [gold.org how much gold](https://www.gold.org/about-gold/gold-supply/gold-mining/how-much-gold-has-been-mined)
- Gold still in the ground (reserves): 66,000 t, USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026. [USGS gold 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-gold.pdf). Wording "all the gold still buried underground" = known **reserves**; say so in the description.
- World population 2026 ≈ 8.3 billion (UN WPP 2024 projection). [Census / UN](https://cdn.www.census.gov/newsroom/stories/world-population-day.html)
- Gold density 19.32 g/cm³.

**Scale maths.**
- 220,000 t ÷ 19.32 t/m³ = 11,387 m³ → edge **22.5 m**.
- +66,000 t = 286,000 t (**+30 %** mass) → 14,803 m³ → edge **24.55 m**. That is **+2.05 m, +9 %**, because the edge grows with the cube root (1.30^(1/3) = 1.09).
- 286,000 t ÷ 8.3 billion = **34.5 g** each → 1.78 cm³ → edge **1.2 cm**, smaller than a typical sugar cube (≈1.5–2 cm).
- 22.5 m ≈ the length of a tennis court (23.8 m) or a 7-storey building. It fits easily under the Eiffel Tower (125 m square base, first platform at 57 m).

**Sync.** Each number appears on its spoken word: "220,000" at "two hundred twenty thousand"; tape "22.5 m" at "twenty-two and a half"; "+30%" at "thirty percent"; "24.6 m" at "two meters"; "8.3 billion" at "eight point three"; "35 g" at "thirty-five grams"; sugar-cube contrast at "sugar cube".

**Readability.** Captions are white heavy grotesque (Poppins ExtraBold / Montserrat Black), ~64–72 px, black outline 6 px plus a soft shadow, at most two lines. Keep them in the zone y ≈ 250–1350 px: the bottom ~420 px and right ~140 px are left free for the Shorts UI. Key numbers sit next to their object in yellow.

**Sound plan.**
- Voice −14 LUFS integrated.
- Minimal electronic bed (soft pulse + plucks, no vocals) ducked about −12 dB under speech.
- Material SFX only on events: ring ting, liquid pour, coin cascade, mould thud, drill, conveyor, many tiny "tinks", pigeon.
- One comic button at the end. No whoosh on every cut.
