## Description
What if every person on Earth lived under one roof?
A town in Alaska already does it on a small scale. Stadiums, mosques and mega-factories hold millions — but not billions.
Pack people shoulder to shoulder, four per square meter, stack them floor after floor, and all 8 billion of us fit in a cube about 1.5 km on each side. Drop it on Manhattan: wider than Central Park, still on the island, and taller than the Burj Khalifa.

## Fact check
- Whittier, Alaska: ~270 residents, most live in the 14-story Begich Towers (post office, police, clinic, church inside). ✔
- Indianapolis Motor Speedway: permanent seating ~250,000 (often cited 235,000–257,000). ✔ "about a quarter of a million"
- Masjid al-Haram: capacity around 2+ million during Hajj (figures up to 2.5 million). ✔
- Giga Texas: ~10 million sq ft ≈ 0.93 km² of floor space; × 4 people/m² ≈ 3.7 million. ✔
- Boeing Everett: largest building in the world by volume, ~13.3–13.4 million m³; at 2.42 people/m³ ≈ 32 million. ✔
- 8 billion ÷ 2.42 people/m³ ≈ 3.3 km³ → cube ≈ 1.49 km per side. ✔ (thought experiment: standing room only, floors 1.65 m high)
- Central Park ≈ 4 km × 0.8 km; Manhattan ≈ 3+ km wide at that latitude, so a 1.5 km cube is wider than the park and fits on the island. ✔
- Burj Khalifa 828 m; Empire State 443 m (tip); Eiffel 330 m. ✔
- Walking 1.5 km at ~5 km/h ≈ 18–20 minutes. ✔
- Satellite imagery: NASA Blue Marble (GIBS) and USGS National Map orthoimagery (public domain). The USGS image of the Giga Texas site predates the factory, so the video shows it only at regional zoom.
