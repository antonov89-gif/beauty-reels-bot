## Verdict
- Right now (momentum): Elizabeth is hotter: 4.5x Chloe's weekly views and 12x the weekly subscribers. This is a novelty peak 2 weeks old, not yet a business.
- Long term and for monetisation (our main goal): Chloe's model is more promising. It is monetized, earns from long vlogs (~99 % of the estimated revenue), has an unlimited topic space, and fits our rules. Its breakout came with the first long vlogs (Rome 23.03, Titanic 14.04), not with Shorts alone.
- Its weakness is a slowing curve and copycats, so we need our own engine, not a copy.

## What we take
From Chloe (the base): a fictional fixed presenter; Shorts as the funnel (now 2-3 min multi-era stories); a long 10-15 min vlog every 2-3 weeks as the money layer; AI mark on screen.
From Elizabeth (the energy): a sharper character voice (attitude, catchphrases), one gross or surprising fact per scene, one physical gag, the next episode named at the end.
Our own: the "What Did It Cost?" data layer (real price lists, hours-of-work chips) = original value and differentiation from the copycats; background anachronism QC.
Recommended series for the history POV channel: story A "What Did It Cost?" (ref_chloe one_link_studio.md), with a long vlog version after 4-6 Shorts. "The Queen's Taster" can be a second series later, never with a real queen on screen.
