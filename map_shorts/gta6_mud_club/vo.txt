Thirty-four days until GTA 6. And this place from the trailer might be real.
It's the Thrillbilly Mud Club: ATVs, swamp buggies and monster trucks, all covered in mud.
Rockstar hasn't said what it's based on, but fans point to Redneck Mud Park in Punta Gorda, Florida.
It's a real off-road park of roughly eight hundred acres, with huge events.
And the swampy region around it, Grassrivers, is widely reported to be the Everglades.
