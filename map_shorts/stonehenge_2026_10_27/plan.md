## Script v1 (EN, ~30 s, voice David as in Sullivan/liver)
1. Four and a half thousand years ago, people built this with stone hammers and rope.
2. The giant stones came from a forest twenty-five kilometres away, and each one weighs about twenty-five tonnes.
3. Archaeologists think teams dragged them on wooden sledges over timber rails.
4. To stand one up, they slid it into a deep pit and hauled it upright with ropes.
5. Then the stones on top were locked in place with joints copied from woodwork.
6. The smaller stones came from Wales, over two hundred kilometres away.
7. And one stone came all the way from Scotland. How they moved it, nobody knows. How would you do it?
