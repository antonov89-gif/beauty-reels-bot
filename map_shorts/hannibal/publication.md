## Description
Why did a general march war elephants over the Alps, straight into the snow? 🐘 In 218 BC, Hannibal of Carthage wanted to defeat Rome, but Rome ruled the sea, so he went by land. By ancient estimates he left Spain with about 90,000 soldiers and 37 war elephants, crossed the Pyrenees and the Rhone, then the Alps, and reached Italy roughly fifteen days later. By ancient accounts only around 26,000 men made it. He still won famous victories, including Cannae in 216 BC, but never took Rome, and was finally beaten at Zama. Numbers and the exact Alpine pass are debated by historians.
