Settings: Veo 3.1 (Fast is enough), 9:16, 720p or 1080p, audio ON, 6 s per clip (or 8 s for the single variant).

## Clip A (text to video, 6 s)
Vertical handheld smartphone selfie video, one continuous take with no cuts, filmed in a dark modern cinema auditorium, photorealistic, natural handheld shake. 0-2.5 s: a young man in a dark denim jacket sits in a red cinema seat in the foreground, holding a clear plastic water bottle, calm, almost no motion; behind him a huge curved screen shows a calm deep blue ocean; soft blue LED strips glow along the aisle; the room is dim and quiet; a low cinematic drone hum. 2.5-4 s: he slowly turns the phone toward the screen; the surface of the screen starts to ripple like water; the bass rises. 4-6 s: a humpback whale pushes its enormous head and long white pectoral fins out through the screen into the room; a wave of real water and white foam bursts into the front rows; droplets splash on the camera lens; bokeh lights; dark silhouettes of the audience scream. No text, no logos.

## Clip B (image to video from the LAST FRAME of clip A, 6 s)
Continue the same handheld take. The whole humpback whale glides out over the audience about three metres above the seats, its belly and barnacles visible; foam and water flood the aisles and cover the red seats; many people in the rows raise their phones to film; the camera swings back to the man, soaked, laughing, pointing up at the whale floating above him, the water bottle still in his hand. Blue aisle LEDs, bokeh, droplets on the lens, real water and foam physics, natural handheld motion. No text, no logos.

## Single clip variant (8 s, if you do not want to join two clips)
Same as clip A, but add: "6-8 s: the whole whale glides over the rows above the audience, people raise their phones, the camera whips back to the man, soaked and laughing, pointing up at the whale."

## Negative prompt
text, captions, subtitles, logos, watermark, brand names, cartoon, anime, plastic skin, distorted hands, extra fingers, cuts, split screen, whale staying inside the screen

## After generation
Join A + B with a short crossfade, add an on-screen "AI-generated" label, then the authenticity check runs on the final file.
