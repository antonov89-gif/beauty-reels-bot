## Facts (opened 26.10)
- Adult living donation: about 50-60% of the donor's liver is removed (children: ~25%). UPMC living-donor process page. VERIFIED
- Both the donor's remnant and the transplanted part grow back to normal/full size; usually the right lobe in adults. UPMC + Johns Hopkins. VERIFIED
- Time: UPMC "about 8 to 10 weeks" (another UPMC FAQ: "within a few months") -> say "in about two months". VERIFIED (range)
- Shape is not restored; mass is. Human paper: "shape is not restored but neighbouring organs mould the remnant so it almost resembles the original"; rodent work: architecture remodelled. -> say "not quite the same shape". PARTLY (older human source + animal data) - hedged
- Normally liver cells (hepatocytes) rarely divide; after resection they divide and enlarge. Reviews (PMC). VERIFIED
- Not claimed: "only organ that regrows", any death/risk numbers.
