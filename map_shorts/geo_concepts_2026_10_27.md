## Evidence
- vidIQ outliers (Shorts, last 3 months, ≥1M views): 70 videos on geography/maps/places. Nexlev: 20 geography Shorts channels with median ≥100k.

### Why we stall at ~1.5k (HYP from our data)
1. Topic too niche (one exclave, one city) -> small audience; million-view topics touch EVERY viewer (their country, the whole world, a route they can imagine).
2. Payoff is verbal (fact told), not visual (map does something surprising).
3. No participation: the viewer has no reason to comment except to correct us.
4. No series identity -> 0.13% subscribe.

## Best: concept upgrade "Map does the twist" (15-35 s)
Formula, one per video:
1. 0 s: question about the WHOLE viewer base on screen + map already moving ("Can you walk from London to Tokyo?").
2. 2-20 s: ONE visual payoff done by the map itself (route grows with a day counter / country dragged and shrinks / circle fills / split line).
3. Last 3 s: participation prompt in text ("Find your country 👇" / "Which one surprised you?") + loop back to frame 0.
4. Series label in the corner (#7) and the same opening sting -> subscribe reason.
5. Own graphics + Esri-free imagery (NASA Blue Marble, Natural Earth) + own music = clean for YPP.

### Top 5 series to launch (ranked)
1. **"Walk It"** (#1, 24/25): "Can you walk from X to Y?" - route on a real globe, km + days counter, blocked by sea/war/jungle twist. Proven 7-61M; fully our engine. First: London -> Cape Town, Lisbon -> Singapore, Alaska -> Patagonia.
2. **"True Size"** (#2 + #5, 24/23): drag a country over another / the Moon over countries; size number on screen. Proven 1.4-7.3M on channels under 5k subs. First: Africa vs everything, Moon vs Russia, Greenland vs Africa (re-cut of our Australia vs Greenland).
3. **"Closer Than You Think"** (#3 + #17 + #26, 24/22/21): guess-first quiz: "Which country is closest to the US?" -> answer reveal on the map. Comment trigger built in.
4. **"Your Country In One Map"** (#4 + #9 + #10, 24/23/23): one data map everyone checks (same latitude, what year it is, time zones). Our Time zones Short already had the best comment rate.
5. **"Weird Borders #N"** (#6, 23): keep, but shorter (≤30 s) and with a map twist instead of narration.

Pause: single-city stories, money/scale, GTA, long videos (unchanged from 25.10). Avoid: #48-50 (YPP risk).
