## Checks
- Duration: Kokoro 31.0 s at 1.1x (ElevenLabs David ~1.05 expected 31-33 s). Re-time after the real take.
- Facts: Mid-Atlantic Ridge average spreading ~2.5 cm/yr (Wikipedia). Fingernails ~3.5 mm/month = ~4.2 cm/yr (Wikipedia) -> "slower than your fingernails" true. 1492 -> 2026 = 534 yr x 2.5 cm = 13.35 m -> "about 13 m"; typical city bus ~12 m -> "longer than a city bus". Pangaea began breaking apart ~175 million years ago (Wikipedia). Iceland sits on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge above sea level.
- Not used: "Hawaii is moving to Japan" (not supported by the Pacific Plate article; the Pacific Plate moves 5.6-10.2 cm/yr, faster than nails, would break the comparison).
- Scale sanity: 175 Myr x 2.5 cm/yr = ~4,400 km, the same order as the Atlantic width. Rates are averages; they vary along the ridge.
- Sync: every number appears on the word that says it (word timestamps from the voice alignment).
- Readability: captions max 2 lines, white heavy grotesk with dark outline, kept out of the Shorts UI zones (top 200 px, bottom 420 px, right 140 px).
