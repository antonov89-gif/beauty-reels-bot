# Louis XIV: our own "body truth" hooks (owner 30.10: "найди ещё отличительные черты (крючок), как зубы, только у нас")
The reference's engine is the ugly truth of Elizabeth's face (lead paint, smallpox scars, rotten teeth). Ours must be our own, so TEETH ARE NOT OUR MAIN HOOK (it is theirs), even though Louis has a teeth story too.
Our engine (fits the character): **"my body fails, I declare it the fashion"**. A vain, handsome king whose body betrays him; every defect becomes a court trend. It continues the episode-1 throughline ("One day, everybody will wear this. I'm just early").

## Visual signature that only we have (proposal)
- The king never lets the phone see him from below his chin (vanity), but his body "leaks" into the frame: a bandage, a cushion, a plate, an open window, a velvet chair; the gag is always what is AT THE EDGE of the frame while he talks about his beauty.
- The court copies every defect: in each episode a courtier in the background imitates the king's newest "fashion" (limp, cushion, bandage, peas). Recurring, cheap, recognisable.

## Rule
Before any of these goes into a script: two sources, primary text where possible (Journal de la santé du roi; Liselotte's letters; Dionis; Saint-Simon), facts hedged in the chip, and the description says it is a comedy reconstruction.
