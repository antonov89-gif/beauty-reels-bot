## 3. Individual recommendations for ours (by expected effect on views; my judgement)
Change:
1. First 3 s: a question or a stake plus a reason to film (free, C01 rewrite). Ours must not be only "I'm handsome".
2. Put the "my body fails, I declare it the fashion" payoff into ep.1 by 40 s (hair loss is there; add one sly confession per 15-20 s); the vanity tags max one per two clips (free, lines).
3. One quotable line per 15-20 s that a viewer would paste into a comment (C12 "borrowed their glassmakers" is the model).
4. Keep 1:55-2:05 for ep.1 (reference range 1:55-2:23); test one 45-60 s cut later.
5. One voice per episode: check every r2v clip against C10 (similarity >= 0.85), re-roll only on a yes.
Add:
6. A recurring segment that is ours alone: "the court copies the newest defect" (a courtier in the background imitates it) in EVERY episode; the viewers wait for it.
7. A reply engine at the end: ask the viewers to choose between two next topics (our own wording, not the reference's "I read them all"); pin a comment; this drives comments.
8. Series chain: every episode ends on the next topic and that episode really comes out within days; cadence 3 per week (about $50 per week of generation at $15-17 per episode; the reference ran about 4 per week).
9. Channel package before the first upload: own account (NOT Mr. Buck; Zernio has only Mr. Buck connected), name, avatar, banner, series tag in every title "(Louis XIV Vlog)", AI-content disclosure on, description saying it is a comedy reconstruction with facts and sources; TikTok and Reels copies from day one.
10. After 5-6 Shorts: an 8-10 min compilation "A day at Versailles" for watch hours.
11. Test (zero cost): burned-in captions on episode 2. The reference vlog 1 has bold white upper-case captions; our rule since 29.10 is none; the owner decides.
12. Mix of hooks across the first five episodes: dinner in public (appetite, ep.2), the operation (ep.3), perfume and shirts (ep.4); the first three decide whether the series is picked up.

## 4. Views vs monetisation (main goal in CLAUDE.md)
- YPP (YouTube Help, 30.10): 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months OR 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days; Shorts-feed watch hours do not count towards the 4,000. A separate Help page says the entry bar rises on 1 Feb 2027 to 8,000 hours / 20M Shorts views (existing members not affected): about 3 months from today. Realistic path: many Shorts for discovery + long compilations for watch hours; 10M Shorts views in 90 days from zero is rare.
- The reference is not monetised at 67k subscribers: views alone do not buy the review. Our protection: original script and character, AI and voice disclosed, facts with two sources, no reused footage, no mass-produced template (vary the setting and the hook every episode).
- Money check: 12 clips left at the Lite rate = about $15; 3 episodes per week = about $50 per week with no income until YPP: decide the budget ceiling before the cadence.
