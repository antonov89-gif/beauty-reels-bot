## Verdict
The character is strong and consistent (vanity, contempt, deadpan buttons: "Naturally", "of course", "To myself, mostly"). The opener and the outro are solid series signatures. Weak points: (1) the episode is a row of brags with no throughline; (2) in three beats it is unclear WHO he is talking to; (3) two repetitions ("a hundred people", "Lower") flatten the jokes; (4) one line is ambiguous by ear ("Careful, that's a hat"). All fixable in C07-C15 (not filmed yet) for about 0.3 RUB of voice per changed line.

## Throughline (SHOULD, free: action and edit only)

setup C04 (hair loss, "I took it personally") -> C06 the white wig, "One day, everybody will wear this. Add one visual payoff (COULD, part of the C12 frame/prompt): in the Hall of Mirrors a few courtiers in the back already wear copies of his tall wig ("everybody will wear this" comes true in one morning).
