## Take 1. Selfie at the table (0:00-0:22)
First frame: the phone far too close, nose and lips fill the frame, tilted, the top half covered by his own scarlet sleeve and fist. A second of fumbling, then he pulls the phone back to his face: he sits at the head of the long table, napkin tucked into the lace, the crowd behind him.
LOUIS: "Bonjour. I am Louis, King of France. And this is my channel. They call me the Sun King. Yes... I'm that handsome. [pause] You say I eat too much. [pause] Of course, that's not true. [pause] Every day I feast in front of the court. Today... before your eyes."
(«Бонжур. Я Людовик, король Франции. И это мой канал. Меня называют Король-Солнце. Да... я настолько красив. Вы говорите, что я слишком много ем. Это, конечно, неправда. Каждый день я пирую на глазах у двора. Сегодня... на ваших.»)
Plate: VERSAILLES, FRANCE · 1685

## Take 2. The room and the rule (0:22-0:49)
Selfie; people come in through a side door and bow, one too little. Then he hands the phone to Marcel, who shows the room: the long table, duchesses on folding stools in front, the crowd standing behind. Back to the king.
LOUIS: "Every night at ten, I dine here. In public. [pause] Anyone dressed properly may come in. [pause] Most of you. [to the late bower] Bow. Lower. Good. [pause] Marcel, show them. [Marcel's wide shot] The duchesses sit. Everyone else stands. [back to the king] Why in public? [pause] A king belongs to his people. Even at dinner."
(«Каждый вечер в десять я ужинаю здесь. При всех. Войти может любой, кто прилично одет. Большинство из вас. Поклон. Ниже. Хорошо. Марсель, покажи им. Герцогини сидят. Остальные стоят. Почему при всех? Король принадлежит своему народу. Даже за ужином.»)
Plates: THE KING'S PUBLIC DINNER · EVERY NIGHT AT 10 PM / A FEW HIGH-RANKING LADIES SAT, THE REST STOOD

## Take 3. The food (0:49-1:19)
Selfie. A stack of empty soup bowls beside him; a servant puts down another. Then a pheasant and a partridge are carried in; he takes a leg with his fingers; Marcel's fork comes into the lens and is pushed away; the greasy leg points at the lens.
LOUIS: "My dinner comes in four rounds of dishes. [pause] First, soup. [pause] My sister-in-law writes that I eat four bowls. [a sixth bowl is put down] She can't count. [pause] Then the roasts. A whole pheasant. A partridge. [Marcel's fork in the lens] Someone taught the peasants to use a fork. [pause] I've untaught them. [leg to the lens] Fork or fingers? Tell me in the comments. [pause] Wrong answers go to the Bastille."
(«Мой ужин приносят в четыре захода. Сначала суп. Невестка пишет, что я съедаю четыре тарелки. Она не умеет считать. Потом жаркое. Фазан целиком. Куропатка. Людишек научили есть вилкой. Я их разучил. Вилка или пальцы? Напишите в комментариях. Неправильные ответы поедут в Бастилию.»)
Plates: FOUR ROUNDS OF DISHES: SOUP, ROASTS AND SALADS, PUDDINGS, FRUIT / HIS SISTER-IN-LAW WROTE: 4 SOUPS, A WHOLE PHEASANT, A PARTRIDGE... / HE FORBADE HIS GRANDSONS TO USE FORKS

## Take 4. The drink (1:19-1:44)
Selfie. He lifts one finger without looking; behind him a gold cup goes from hand to hand. It reaches him, Marcel bumps the table, wine splashes over the lens. He laughs, turns the red phone onto Marcel, who freezes.
LOUIS: "There is no glass on my table. [pause] When I want a drink, I ask..."
CUPBEARER (off screen): "A drink for the King!"
LOUIS: "...and I get a little older. [wine on the lens; he laughs] Mon Dieu. [pause] Leave it. Nobody saw anything. [pause] And nobody will see you again. [Marcel's terrified face; pause] I'm joking."
MARCEL (quietly): "Sorry, Sire."
(«Бокала на моём столе нет. Хочу пить, прошу...» Виночерпий: «Питьё для короля!» «...и немного старею. Боже мой. Оставь. Никто ничего не видел. И тебя больше никто не увидит. Шучу.» Марсель: «Простите, сир.»)
Plate: GLASSES WERE BROUGHT ONLY WHEN THE KING ASKED

## Take 5. The last services (1:44-2:02)
Quick close-ups of the dishes (salad, mutton, ham, pastry, fruit) and a small silver dish of green peas; he holds one pea up to the lens.
LOUIS: "Then salad. Ham. Pastry. Fruit. [pause] All in less than an hour. [pause] And peas. The whole court talks about nothing else."
Then Marcel holds the phone and shows the whole laden table, and the king eating with great appetite: he tears bread, bites into a pastry, licks his fingers, takes a spoonful of peas, chews with his eyes closed in bliss (about 4 s, no words, only chewing and the crowd's murmur). Then he leans back in the armchair, hands on his belly, eyes half-closed, a long happy sigh.
LOUIS: "Ohh... [pause] I wasn't born to work. [pause] I was born for happiness, love, pleasure... and a good rest. [a joyful, delighted laugh at his own witty line, as if he has just made the best joke at court]"
(«Потом салат. Ветчина. Выпечка. Фрукты. Всё меньше чем за час. И горошек. Весь двор только о нём и говорит.» Марсель показывает весь стол и как король сытно ест, около 4 секунд без слов. Король откидывается в кресле: «Ох... Не для работы создан я, а для счастья, любви, удовольствия и отдыха.» [радостный смех: он сам смеётся над своей остроумной шуткой])
Plates: THE MEAL LASTED LESS THAN AN HOUR / A LETTER FROM THE COURT: EVERYONE WAS CRAZY ABOUT GREEN PEAS

## Take 6. The verdict (2:02-2:16)
He sits up, wipes his fingers, looks at the pheasant bones on his plate, then into the lens, chin up.
LOUIS: "So. Do I eat too much? [pause] No. I eat like a king. [pause] Don't be afraid of getting fat. Be afraid of being a loser. [haughty laugh]"
(«Итак. Я слишком много ем? Нет. Я ем как король. Не бойтесь потолстеть, бойтесь остаться неудачниками.»)

## Take 7. Next episode and the signature outro (2:16-2:42)
He stands, the crowd bows, he looks into the hand mirror, nods, points at the lens; then orders with a finger, waves the viewers off, laughs coldly and sits back down at the full table.
LOUIS: "All this food has a price. [pause] In the next video, I'll tell you about my operation. [pause] My surgeon practised it first... on other patients. [pause] Very kind of them. [pause] Subscribe to my stunning channel. [pause] Hit like. Ring the bell. Anyway, do what you like. [haughty laugh] I'm off to the table to feast!"
(«Вся эта еда имеет цену. В следующем видео расскажу о своей операции. Мой хирург сначала тренировался... на других пациентах. Очень мило с их стороны. Подписывайтесь на мой потрясающий канал. Ставьте лайк. Жмите на колокольчик. А впрочем, делайте что хотите. А я пошёл к столу пировать!»)
Plate: NEXT: THE OPERATION.

## Take 8. He cannot stop the recording (2:42-2:48)
At the table he turns the phone to his face and pokes the screen to stop the video. Nothing happens. He frowns, pokes again, harder; his finger fills the lens, the picture shakes and tilts, his eye comes very close to the lens.
LOUIS: "So... [pause] how do you turn this off?"
(«Так... как тут выключить?»)
Hard cut mid-poke to the end card.
End card: THE SUN IS NEVER LATE.
