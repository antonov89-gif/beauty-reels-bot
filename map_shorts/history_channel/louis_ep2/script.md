# Louis XIV Vlog #2 "Dinner in Public": script (draft 1, 31.10.2026)
About 2:05. Evening, Versailles, the king's public dinner. He films himself on a phone, as in episode 1. Lines are spoken in English; the Russian is for the owner only. No music.

## Setup 1. The antechamber, candles (0:00-0:27)

**C01 (0:00-0:11)**
The phone looks at the painted ceiling, candle smoke drifts across it. He turns the phone onto himself; he is already seated at a long table, napkin tucked into the lace.
LOUIS: "Oops, the ceiling. There. Bonjour. I am Louis, King of France. They call me the Sun King. Yes... I'm that handsome."
(«Ой, потолок. Вот. Бонжур. Я Людовик, король Франции. Меня называют Король-Солнце. Да... я настолько красив.»)
Plate: VERSAILLES, FRANCE · 1685

**C02 (0:11-0:19)**
He tilts the phone to show the empty gold plates in front of him, then brings it back to his face.
LOUIS: "So. Every night at ten, I have dinner in front of the whole court. Tonight... that's you too."
(«Итак. Каждый вечер в десять я ужинаю на глазах у всего двора. Сегодня... и у вас тоже.»)
Plate: THE KING'S PUBLIC DINNER · EVERY NIGHT AT 10 PM

**C03 (0:19-0:27)**
Behind him people come in through a side door and bow. One bows too little; he looks over his shoulder at him.
LOUIS: "Bow. Lower. Good. [pause] Anyone who's dressed properly can come in and watch me eat."
(«Поклон. Ниже. Хорошо. Любой, кто прилично одет, может зайти и смотреть, как я ем.»)

## Setup 2. The first course (0:27-0:53)

**C04 (0:27-0:35)**
The camera sags to the front row: ladies on folding stools, the crowd standing behind them. He lifts it back up.
LOUIS: "The duchesses get the stools. Everybody else stands. [pause] Someone back there is counting my bites."
(«Герцогиням положены табуреты. Остальные стоят. Кто-то там сзади считает, сколько я откусил.»)
Plate: A FEW HIGH-RANKING LADIES SAT IN FRONT, THE REST STOOD

**C05 (0:35-0:44)**
A servant puts down soup. He eats it straight from the bowl, puts it aside, a second bowl comes at once.
LOUIS: "My sister-in-law writes letters about my dinners. Four bowls of soup, she says. [pause] She stopped counting early."
(«Невестка пишет письма о моих ужинах. Четыре тарелки супа, говорит. Она рано бросила считать.»)
Plate: HIS SISTER-IN-LAW WROTE: 4 SOUPS, A WHOLE PHEASANT, A PARTRIDGE, AND MORE

**C06 (0:44-0:53)**
A pheasant arrives. The page Marcel holds out a silver fork right into the lens. Louis takes a leg with his fingers.
MARCEL (off screen): "Your fork, Sire?"
LOUIS: "No, Marcel. My grandsons learned to use a fork. I banned it. [pause] I've got ten fingers."
(Марсель: «Ваша вилка, сир?» Людовик: «Нет, Марсель. Внуков научили есть вилкой. Я запретил. У меня десять пальцев.»)
Plate: HE FORBADE HIS GRANDSONS TO USE FORKS

## Setup 3. The drink (0:53-1:19)

**C07 (0:53-1:01)**
He points the phone over his shoulder: in the crowd a courtier is eating a chicken leg with his hands and licking his fingers, very seriously.
LOUIS: "Look, back there. He eats with his fingers now. [pause] Last week he had a silver fork."
(«Смотрите, вон там. Теперь он ест руками. А на прошлой неделе у него была серебряная вилка.»)

**C08 (1:01-1:10)**
He lifts one finger without looking. Behind him a cup starts its trip from hand to hand.
CUPBEARER (off screen): "A drink for the King!"
LOUIS: "There's no glass on my table. When I want a drink, I ask... [pause] and then I wait."
(Виночерпий: «Питьё для короля!» Людовик: «Бокала на моём столе нет. Хочу пить, прошу... а потом жду.»)
Plate: GLASSES WERE BROUGHT ONLY WHEN THE KING ASKED

**C09 (1:10-1:19)**
The cup reaches him at last, the page bumps the table, wine splashes over the lens. A red blur, silence. Louis laughs, for real, at himself.
MARCEL (quietly): "Sorry, Sire."
LOUIS: "[laughs] Mon Dieu, it's all over the phone. [pause] Leave it. Nobody saw anything."
(Марсель: «Простите, сир.» Людовик: «[смеётся] Боже мой, всё на телефоне. Оставь. Никто ничего не видел.»)

## Burst. The courses (1:19-1:27)
Eight close-ups of about 1 s, no words: soup, pheasant, partridge, a big salad, mutton, ham, pastry, fruit. Only plates, silver, the crowd murmuring.
Plate: SOUP, ROASTS, SALADS, SWEETS, THEN FRUIT

## Setup 4. Dessert (1:27-1:43)

**C10 (1:27-1:35)**
A small silver dish of green peas. He holds one pea up to the lens, focus hunts.
LOUIS: "Peas. A lady here wrote that the court talks about nothing else. [pause] I started that."
(«Горошек. Одна дама при дворе написала, что двор только о нём и говорит. Это я начал.»)
Plate: 1696 LETTER: THE COURT'S CRAZE FOR GREEN PEAS

**C11 (1:35-1:43)**
He leans back, wipes his fingers on the napkin, glances at the crowd. Quieter.
LOUIS: "They call it greed. [pause] I call it a long day of being looked at."
(«Они называют это обжорством. А я называю это длинным днём, когда на тебя все смотрят.»)

## Ending (1:43-2:05)

**C12 (1:43-1:53)**
He stands up, the crowd bows. He looks into the hand mirror, nods to himself, then points at the camera.
LOUIS: "Next time, my surgeon has plans for me. [pause] Don't worry, he's been practising. [pause] Subscribe to my stunning channel."
(«В следующий раз у моего хирурга на меня планы. Не волнуйтесь, он тренировался. Подписывайтесь на мой потрясающий канал.»)
Plate: NEXT: THE OPERATION

**C13 (1:53-2:03)**
He gives orders with a finger, waves the viewers off on "do what you like", laughs coldly with his chin up, then turns and sits back down at the full table. The mirror covers the lens.
LOUIS: "Hit like. Ring the bell. Anyway, do what you like. [haughty laugh] I'm off to the table to feast!"
(«Ставьте лайк. Жмите на колокольчик. А впрочем, делайте что хотите. [надменный смех] А я пошёл к столу пировать!»)
