# Louis XIV Vlog #2 "Dinner in Public": script (draft 2, 31.10.2026)
About 2:45. Evening, Versailles, the king's public dinner. He films himself on a phone, as in episode 1; twice he hands the phone to Marcel. He talks to the viewers as if they stood in the crowd. Lines are spoken in English; the Russian is for the owner only. No music.

## Setup 1. The antechamber, candles (0:00-0:28)

**C01 (0:00-0:11)**
The phone looks at the painted ceiling, candle smoke drifts across it. He turns the phone onto himself; he is already seated at a long table, napkin tucked into the lace.
LOUIS: "Oops, the ceiling. There. Bonjour. I am Louis, King of France. They call me the Sun King. Yes... I'm that handsome."
(«Ой, потолок. Вот. Бонжур. Я Людовик, король Франции. Меня называют Король-Солнце. Да... я настолько красив.»)
Plate: VERSAILLES, FRANCE · 1685

**C02 (0:11-0:20)**
He tilts the phone to the empty gold plates, then brings it back close to his face, as if letting the viewer in on something.
LOUIS: "So. Every night at ten, I have dinner in front of the whole court. [pause] Tonight you're in the crowd too. Stand at the back."
(«Итак. Каждый вечер в десять я ужинаю на глазах у всего двора. Сегодня и вы в толпе. Встаньте сзади.»)
Plate: THE KING'S PUBLIC DINNER · EVERY NIGHT AT 10 PM

**C03 (0:20-0:28)**
Behind him people come in through a side door and bow. One bows too little; he looks over his shoulder at him, then back at the lens.
LOUIS: "Bow. Lower. Good. [pause] Anyone dressed properly can come in and watch. [pause] You too. I suppose."
(«Поклон. Ниже. Хорошо. Любой, кто прилично одет, может зайти и смотреть. И вы тоже. Пожалуй.»)

## Setup 2. Marcel films (0:28-0:38)

**C04 (0:28-0:38)**
He holds the phone out to the side without looking. Marcel takes it; the picture jumps, then shows the room wide: the long table, the duchesses on folding stools in front, the crowd standing behind. Marcel tilts down too far: the king's double chin fills the frame.
LOUIS: "Marcel, take it. Show them the room. [pause] The duchesses get the stools. Everyone else stands. [pause] Higher, Marcel. Not my chin."
(«Марсель, держи. Покажи им зал. Герцогиням положены табуреты. Остальные стоят. Выше, Марсель. Не подбородок.»)
Plate: A FEW HIGH-RANKING LADIES SAT IN FRONT, THE REST STOOD

## Setup 3. The first course (0:38-1:13)

**C05 (0:38-0:48)**
The phone is back in his hand. A servant puts down soup; he eats it straight from the bowl, a second bowl comes at once. He holds the full spoon up to the lens.
LOUIS: "My sister-in-law writes letters about my dinners. Four bowls of soup, she says. [pause] Hungry? You may look."
(«Невестка пишет письма о моих ужинах. Четыре тарелки супа, говорит. Проголодались? Смотреть можно.»)
Plate: HIS SISTER-IN-LAW WROTE: 4 SOUPS, A WHOLE PHEASANT, A PARTRIDGE, A BIG SALAD, AND MORE

**C06 (0:48-0:57)**
A pheasant arrives. Marcel holds out a silver fork right into the lens. Louis takes a leg with his fingers.
MARCEL (off screen): "Your fork, Sire?"
LOUIS: "No, Marcel. My grandsons learned to use a fork. I banned it. [pause] I've got ten fingers."
(Марсель: «Ваша вилка, сир?» Людовик: «Нет, Марсель. Внуков научили есть вилкой. Я запретил. У меня десять пальцев.»)
Plate: HE FORBADE HIS GRANDSONS TO USE FORKS

**C07 (0:57-1:05)**
He points the phone over his shoulder: in the crowd a courtier is eating a chicken leg with his hands and licking his fingers, very seriously.
LOUIS: "Look, back there. He eats with his fingers now. [pause] Last week he had a silver fork."
(«Смотрите, вон там. Теперь он ест руками. А на прошлой неделе у него была серебряная вилка.»)

**C08 (1:05-1:13)**
He points the greasy pheasant leg at the lens.
LOUIS: "So, you. Fork or fingers? Tell me in the comments. [pause] Wrong answers go to the Bastille."
(«Итак, вы. Вилка или пальцы? Напишите в комментариях. Неправильные ответы поедут в Бастилию.»)

## Setup 4. The drink (1:13-1:39)

**C09 (1:13-1:21)**
He lifts one finger without looking. Behind him a cup starts its trip from hand to hand.
CUPBEARER (off screen): "A drink for the King!"
LOUIS: "There's no glass on my table. When I want a drink, I ask... [pause] and then I wait."
(Виночерпий: «Питьё для короля!» Людовик: «Бокала на моём столе нет. Хочу пить, прошу... а потом жду.»)
Plate: GLASSES WERE BROUGHT ONLY WHEN THE KING ASKED

**C10 (1:21-1:30)**
The cup reaches him at last, Marcel bumps the table, wine splashes over the lens. A red blur, silence. Louis laughs, for real, at himself.
MARCEL (quietly): "Sorry, Sire."
LOUIS: "[laughs] Mon Dieu, it's all over the phone. [pause] Leave it. Nobody saw anything."
(Марсель: «Простите, сир.» Людовик: «[смеётся] Боже мой, всё на телефоне. Оставь. Никто ничего не видел.»)

**C11 (1:30-1:39)**
He wipes the lens with his sleeve and turns the phone towards Marcel, who reads the comments on the screen over his shoulder.
LOUIS: "Marcel, what are they saying?"
MARCEL: "One asks if you ever eat a vegetable, Sire."
LOUIS: "Salad. A whole bowl. [pause] Write that down."
(«Марсель, что они пишут?» Марсель: «Один спрашивает, едите ли вы хоть когда-нибудь овощи, сир.» Людовик: «Салат. Целую миску. Запиши.»)

## Burst. The courses (1:39-1:47)
Eight close-ups of about 1 s, no words: soup, pheasant, partridge, a big salad, mutton, ham, pastry, fruit. Only plates, silver, the crowd murmuring.
Plate: SOUP, ROASTS, SALADS, SWEETS, THEN FRUIT

## Setup 5. Dessert (1:47-2:20)

**C12 (1:47-1:56)**
A small silver dish of green peas. He holds one pea up to the lens, focus hunts.
LOUIS: "Peas. A lady here wrote that the court talks about nothing else. [pause] I started that. Try them."
(«Горошек. Одна дама при дворе написала, что двор только о нём и говорит. Это я начал. Попробуйте.»)
Plate: 1696 LETTER: THE COURT'S CRAZE FOR GREEN PEAS

**C13 (1:56-2:08)**
He leans back, wipes his fingers on the napkin, glances at the crowd. Quieter. Then he looks straight into the lens, chin up.
LOUIS: "They call it greed. [pause] I call it a long day of being looked at. [pause] Don't be afraid of getting fat. Be afraid of being a loser. [haughty laugh]"
(«Они называют это обжорством. А я называю это длинным днём, когда на тебя все смотрят. Не бойтесь потолстеть, бойтесь остаться неудачниками. [надменный смех]»)

**C14 (2:08-2:20)**
At the edge of the frame Marcel's hand slowly reaches for the last partridge leg. Louis sees it on the screen, freezes, looks at him.
LOUIS: "Marcel. [pause] Put it down."
MARCEL: "I haven't eaten since this morning, Sire."
LOUIS: "Neither have they. [nods at the crowd] [pause] Fine. Take a pea. One."
(«Марсель. Положи.» Марсель: «Я с утра не ел, сир.» Людовик: «Они тоже. Ладно. Возьми горошину. Одну.»)

## Ending (2:20-2:44)

**C15 (2:20-2:31)**
He stands up, the crowd bows. He looks into the hand mirror, nods to himself, then points at the camera.
LOUIS: "Next time, you choose. My surgeon, or my bath. [pause] Actually, I choose. The surgeon. He's been practising. [pause] Subscribe to my stunning channel."
(«В следующий раз выбираете вы. Мой хирург или моя ванна. Хотя нет, выбираю я. Хирург. Он тренировался. Подписывайтесь на мой потрясающий канал.»)
Plate: NEXT: THE OPERATION

**C16 (2:31-2:41)**
He gives orders with a finger, waves the viewers off on "do what you like", laughs coldly with his chin up, then turns and sits back down at the full table. The mirror covers the lens.
LOUIS: "Hit like. Ring the bell. Anyway, do what you like. [haughty laugh] I'm off to the table to feast!"
(«Ставьте лайк. Жмите на колокольчик. А впрочем, делайте что хотите. [надменный смех] А я пошёл к столу пировать!»)
