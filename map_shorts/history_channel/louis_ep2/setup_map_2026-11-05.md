# Camera setups for ep.2, built on the reference's camera grammar (05.11, owner: "почему опять один и тот же кадр короля... в референсе разные ракурсы")
Fact check of my own work: 11 generation scripts of 04-05.11 (pilot, pieces 2/2b/3/3 alive, T1 v4, T1 anchored, T2a, T2a v2, pose stills, T2b still) used the SAME image T1_keyframe_edit_768.jpg (one selfie medium, eye level, hand at the wig). The reference (frames_vlog1_LjfGP7wpjVs.md, measured on frames) has 7 different setups in 128 s: (1) extreme low selfie with a thumb, tilting up to a propped eye-level medium; (2) table-height, rolled (dutch), very close, profile; (3) very low from the table looking up, push-in to an extreme close-up, fingers in the foreground; (4) a 6 s burst of 9 food close-ups; (5) side-on hand-held medium close-up, push-in to the servant; (6) wide vignetted view from low; (7) new spot, propped eye level, a thumb covers the lens as the transition. My error: I analysed this and then kept one setup for everything.

## Rule from now on
- Every take has its own camera setup(s); a change of setup is a hard cut (as in the reference), so the first frame of a new setup does NOT have to match the previous clip.
- Each setup gets its own start still: the king's face from the card (A3) plus the matching angle tile from the owner's sheet (K_left34, K_right34, K_profile_left/right, K_front) as identity references, never a re-use of the same picture.
- Inside a setup: first frame and last frame = the same camera, different pose (the rule proven on T2a v2).
- Face gate per clip as before; the profile and low angles score lower on the metric, so the eye check is decisive there.
