
## Confirmed 04.11: kling-4.0-i2v has no identity-lock field, only frame-position images
Per the OpenAPI spec (see the entry above): `image_urls` (first+last, max 2) and `keyframe_urls` (middle, max 8) are all POSITION-bound (a specific moment in time), not identity references. There is no field that says "this is who the character is, keep looking like this the whole clip" independent of a time position. Result on T1: @first_frame pinned frame 0, but the face still drifted to a different-looking man from about 4 s in (owner caught it, I had not checked past frame 0). For any clip longer than a few seconds with no second position-anchored image near the drifting part, expect identity drift; keep single takes short (6-8 s) or add a @keyframe_N anchor at the point that matters.
