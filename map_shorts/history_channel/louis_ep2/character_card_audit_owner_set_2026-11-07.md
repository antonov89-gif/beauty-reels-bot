## Findings
- Identity inside O1-O5: consistent by eye: same face, grey-green eyes, moustache with curled tips, beauty mark on the VIEWER'S RIGHT under the mouth, rouge, same wig shape (tall, side roll curls, long ringlets), scarlet velvet with gold suns, white lace cravat.
- O1-O5 vs A3: 0.65-0.73 (O1 0.69, O3 0.65, O5 0.66 below the 0.70 clip gate). This is a different render of the king, not just a new pose: wig is WARM greige/beige (R-B about 44) while A3 is cooler silver-white (R-B about 16); face is heavier/older (about 45, lines on forehead) than the 35 in face_master_v3 text. Mixing A3 and O-set as refs in one call = two wigs, two ages: do not.
- Face size is 37-40 percent of width: good (>=28).
- Defects as a card: chest cropped at the sleeves (no full costume), no profile/3-4/low/high angles at all (every tile is frontal), O3 eyes closed (useless as identity ref), O2 asymmetric one-eye shock (useful as expression only, tends to carry the asymmetry), O5 hand with rings + pea: pea can be copied into other scenes, ring hand ok as a props ref only. Background grey-brown gradient: fine, clean. No text, no blur.
- Beige wig: our locked wording is 'cool silver-grey and white, NOT beige/cream/golden'. The O-set wig is beige: it contradicts the lock in the bible. Owner's later inline images (cool silver-grey) match the lock; O1-O5 do not.
- Inline-only images: older/heavier face in the ring-hand 3/4 and in the hall selfie = drift in the owner's own generation; the hall selfie has a crystal glass (glass trap). Cannot be measured.

Good as an expression and costume set, NOT yet a complete card: one master candidate, no angles, wig colour must be decided. Do not mix with A3.
