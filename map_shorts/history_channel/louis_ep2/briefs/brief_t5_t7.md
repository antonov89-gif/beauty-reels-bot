## What our AI clips usually get wrong here (PROMPT_CAMERA_LESSONS.md, 4b-A / 4b-B emotion QC of 07.11, T3-T4 motion)

2. Stifled or deadpan beats come out as a grin or soft smile from second 0 (4b-B). Fix: "face flat, lips pressed, no smile" until the stated second; the smile switches off in 0.3 s.
3. Blur hides the key action 1.5 s early (4b-A). Fix: blur as an event with its second, after the action.
4. Thumb 20-35 % of the width, near the face. Fix: fingertip 12 % (max 20 %), 0.6 s, bottom right, late slot.
5. Room is static (left side especially), one person moves. Fix: three named people on the right/centre with seconds, every 2-3 s.
6. The camera calms after 5 s, long takes on one angle. Fix: absolute per-2-s numbers plus a keep-in-frame clause and one re-aim at mid-take; cut the long takes (6, 7b) in the edit if the raw clip sits.
7. The end pose barely differs from the start; the pin is an eyes-closed pose (5a, 5c). Fix: write the change in verbs and ask the eyes to reopen within 0.8 s.
8. Hands freeze (Marcel's in 4b; ours: the counting hand, the pointing hand). Fix: hand verb per 2 s.
9. Direct address held for 4 s without a gaze change. Fix: gaze list per beat; lens look 0.6-1.0 s once per take, at the stated second.
10. Voice tone: NOT VERIFIED (no sound in the reference sheets or the clips).
