## A. Story and meaning (every episode)
A1 [T] Myth or accusation from the viewers -> a promise ("today I show you") -> a tour where every block is a fact -> a verdict that answers the myth -> a short moral/command to the viewer -> next-episode tease (Vlog 6: "you say I bathed once a month" ... "So, once a month? No." ... "wash your hands, stop believing myths").
A2 [T] Facts 2 : 1 jokes (Vlog 6: about 12 fact blocks, about 6 jokes); jokes grow out of the facts or an accident, never stand alone.
A3 [T] A reason to film in the opener (Vlog 1: "a queen must be seen by her people, so here I am").
A4 [T] A personal hook with a payoff (Tower: "I was here as a prisoner... this time I have the keys"): a past humiliation turned into power.
A5 [T] A confession + a real fact + a turn; one human moment where he laughs at himself; one soft moment, not only arrogance.
A6 [T] Next-episode tease + one self-aware joke at the end.

## B. Lines and delivery
B1 [M/T] Pace 50-90 words per minute (Vlog 2 about 50, Vlog 1 83-99, Vlog 6 about 90); 2.5-3.1 words per speaking second; 7-9 pauses > 0.5 s per 25-30 s take; a beat of silence after each gag.
B2 [T] Short spoken sentences, unfinished thoughts, "so", "well", "and yes", self-corrections ("Why am I upside down? Good. Right."), searching for a word ("you have this now, uh, sauna").
B3 [T] Reactions to what happens in the room, not lines to the lens only ("He is learning.", "It is strong.", "Ow.", a snort, a sigh); talking to the people next to him ("Kate, film me", "Well done, Kate").
B4 [T] Two-word asides after a fact ("Very fancy.", "Very important.", "Do not ask.") - the shape only, never these words.
B5 [T] Joke mechanics (Vlog 2): dramatic irony from a real fact; plain physical words ("tastes like a punishment"); denial right after the evidence; a small retreat that reveals a worse truth; a one-word put-down of the comments; a tiny pain after a long list; a side character answers in one flat word, then silence.
B6 [T] At most one smug tag every two clips; no stock "statement + punchline" pairs; one or two French words per episode.

## C. Camera and angles
C1 [M] A phone in the hand in EVERY shot: drift, small roll, re-aims, tilts, push-ins; the framing never stands still (Vlog 1 seconds with a frozen background 0 %, still hands/body 0 %; head motion 0.20 face widths per second). Owner 05.11: no propped/static shots at all.
C2 [M] Many different angles: in 128 s Vlog 1 has 7 setups: (1) extreme low selfie with a thumb, tilt up to a medium; (2) table-height, rolled (dutch) 10-20 degrees, very close, profile; (3) very low from the table looking up, push-in to an extreme close-up, fingers in the foreground; (4) a burst of food close-ups; (5) side-on hand-held medium close-up; (6) a wide vignetted view from low; (7) a new spot, eye level. Every setup = its own angle and its own start still.
C3 [M] Ultra-wide front camera look: strong perspective at close range (nose enlarged, body small), face and hands fill the frame, the room behind.
C4 [M] Phone mistakes: 5 in 128 s (thumb at the edge 0-3 s, pink thumb lower right 26-34 s, a hand sweeping in from the top 53-55 s, fingers and a toothpick in the foreground with rack focus 76-81 s, a thumb covering the lens 1:51 as the transition into the last scene). At least 1 per take.
C5 [H] Tower of London: one setup per beat of about 8 s, 15 shots in 2:01, walking bob, camera dropping to the skirts, the lens brushing a shoulder and a doorframe, a coin against the lens with focus hunting, a hand reaching to stop the recording at the end.

## D. Editing: shot lengths and cuts
D1 [M] Vlog 1: 14 shots, 13 hard cuts in 128 s; three long takes 23.6 / 28.8 / 30.1 s (64 % of the runtime), three medium shots 9.8 / 12.7 / 17.4 s, one 5.9 s burst of 9 micro-cuts (0.2-1.5 s) of food; mean 9.2 s, median 1.9 s.
D2 [M] No seam inside a long take (face, colour, brightness, sound continuous for 18-30 s); cuts only between setups, never a jump cut inside a setup.
D3 [M] The first 4.5 s of Vlog 1 are one continuous camera move (no cut): the opening is a gesture, not an edit.
D4 [M] A thumb over the lens is used as a transition between scenes.
D5 [H] Tower of London: 15 shots, about 8 s each, all hard cuts (a tour structure).
D6 Our gate per episode: 2-3 long takes 15-30 s, 3-4 medium 8-17 s, one burst of 6-9 cuts of objects/food, cuts only between setups.

## E. People and the room
E1 [M/H] The room lives: in the reference the camera always moves; in a crowded scene the people behind react (Tower: guards speak, react to a lion roar, lean into the selfie).
E2 Our gate: fans, whispering, weight shifts, a servant crossing in every crowd shot (the "living room" paragraph); background never frozen.
E3 [T] Side characters with short lines (Kate, the servant, the guards); the king's deadpan lands on them; one beat of silence after.

## F. Sound
F1 [M] No music. Diegetic bed always: never silence longer than 1 s; loudness about -15.4 LUFS, true peak -1.1 dBFS, LRA 11.1 LU.
F2 [M] Phone handling: about 24 low thumps in 128 s (start and end of takes, mostly under speech), finger rubs, mic scrape.
F3 [M] Phone-mic floor = low rumble 170-624 Hz (not hiss); the floor changes per take.
F4 [M] Proximity: the voice louder and boomier when close, thinner and roomier when far; word level range about 13.8 dB; darker voice (tilt about -8.4 dB/oct, top rolls off about 7 kHz).
F5 [H] Objects and body: clinks, cracks, chewing, sighs, laughter, other people's lines; room echo strongest in wide shots.
F6 Our voice: the locked v3 voice laid over the picture by whole phrases; the model audio muted.

## G. Picture
G1 [M] Reference: 1080x1920, 30 fps; head-area detail about 810 (Laplacian), fine texture 13.4; muted colour (saturation about 96, brightness about 102), natural daylight.
G2 Ours (owner 05.11 keeps 720p, 24 fps): grade in the edit toward saturation about 120-130 and brightness about 120, film grain, a light sharpen; export 1080x1920.
G3 [M] Captions: burned-in white uppercase, small, at about 78 % of the height, words building up. Ours: NO burned-in captions (owner's decision), platform SRT only.

## H. Generation technique learned on ep.2 (to get the above)
H1 Kling 4.0 i2v: name the images @first_frame / @keyframe_N / @last_frame.
H2 First and last frame = the same camera and composition, a different pose (proven on T2a v2); a frame of another composition makes the clip jump or ghost.
H3 Never chain from the model's last frame (detail fell about 80 % in three links); start each clip from a clean still.
H4 A new setup = a new start still from the card A3 + the matching angle tile; never re-use one king still for several setups; references that contain a pose are copied by the model, so use a hall-only crop when only the room is needed.
H5 Gate every clip before showing: face vs the card (SFace median >= 0.65, p10 >= 0.45, eye check), sharpness, no seam spike, frozen-background seconds <= 10 %, still-body seconds <= 15 %, first/last pins held.
