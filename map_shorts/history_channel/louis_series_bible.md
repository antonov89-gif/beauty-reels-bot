# Louis XIV series bible: signature phrases and self-praise rule (owner 28.10.2026)
Rule: every video STARTS with the same signature opener, ENDS with the same signature outro (the CTA-and-feast ending), and all the way through the king admires himself ("what a handsome man I am"). One TTS voice for all lines (see channels.md section 2b). English lines, nasty, arrogant, haughty; ElevenLabs tags per line: [smug], [haughty], [admiringly], [sneering]. The king has a FRENCH ACCENT (owner 29.10; voice choice in voice/accent_plan.md).

## Text style rules (owner 29.10: no traces of AI in the text, clear to an English-speaking audience)
Write like a person talking, not like a model. Contractions (I'm, don't, that's). No em dashes. No "not X, but Y" constructions, no stock punchline pairs, no chains of one-word sentences (one short burst per clip at most). Say plainly what a thing is the first time ("the rising" instead of the French "lever"; no "c." or Latin). Idioms must be ones a US/UK viewer uses. About 15-19 words per 8-s clip. Read-aloud test: if an English friend would not say it, rewrite it. Captions use ordinary spelling. French words limited to the ones English speakers know ("my friends", "no?", "Magnifique").

## Signature opener (fixed text, first 6-7 s of EVERY video, right after "Marcel, down!" in ep.1)
"Bow. Lower. Good. Welcome to Versailles, my friends. Yes, I'm that handsome. Try not to faint."
(The words "Bow. Lower. Good." come back as a call-back in the bowing scene of every episode.)

## Signature outro (fixed text, last ~16 s of EVERY video; owner 29.10)
"Subscribe to my stunning channel. Hit like. Ring the bell. Anyway, do what you like. [haughty, arrogant laugh] I'm off to the table to feast!"
Laugh rule (owner 29.10): the laugh comes AFTER "do what you like" and is cold, arrogant and haughty (chin up, down the nose, eyes half-closed), not a warm giggle. He commands the audience, waves them off with the back of the hand, laughs at them and walks away to a banquet table (mirror flash = cut). The title card "THE SUN IS NEVER LATE." is the on-screen sign-off (not voiced; the voiced "The sun is never late." is retired from the outro and may appear as a line inside an episode).

## Self-praise lines (the "what a handsome man I am" tags)
Rules: one tag every 15-20 s (6 per 2 minutes), never the same line twice in one episode, 2-8 words, always straight to the lens or to the mirror, delivered smugly, never apologetic; no jokes about illness or real people's bodies other than the king's own vanity.
Bank (extend freely): "Look at me. Magnificent, no?" / "What a face, though." / "Handsome again. Every single morning." / "I could cry. Look at that profile." / "White suits me. Everything does." / "Only one worth looking at. Me, obviously." / "Look at that ankle." / "Lucky mirrors." / "Ah... what a profile." / "They're all my good side." / "Magnifique. Me, I mean." / "Sorry. I can't help it." / "Stunning. Who dressed him? A genius." / "Even my shadow is handsome." / "Don't touch. Just admire." / "Perfection takes no effort. Mine, anyway."
Use in ep.1: C03 (Magnificent), C05 (What a face), C07 (white suits me), C08 (Obviously), C10 (ankle), C12 (Lucky mirrors), C13 (what a profile), C14 (a little).

## Fixed cast and props
Marcel the page (off screen, "my left side, MY left"), the gilded hand mirror (cut device), the plumed hat gag, the red heels, the white-grey wig.
