# Recording sheet: the natural (human) voice of Louis XIV (owner 28.10: AI voices are not natural enough)
ONE performer for every video of the channel. Record once, reuse: the signature opener, the outro and the self-praise bank are recorded ONE time and reused in every episode; only new story lines are recorded per episode.

## How he sounds
An insufferable, smug aristocrat who adores his own reflection. Slow, drawling, lifted chin, a smile you can hear. Linger on the vowels of "handsome", "magnificent", "me". Contempt for everyone else in the room, tenderness for himself. Dry, never shouting. Short audible sniffs, sighs and small satisfied laughs ("Hm.") are welcome between lines. One accent for ALL videos (upper-class British, or a light French accent: pick once and keep it). English, not rushed: about 2.3 words per second.

## How to record (phone is fine)
Quiet room with soft furniture (a closet full of clothes works), phone 15-20 cm from the mouth slightly to the side (pop shield: a sock over the mic), no music, no fan. Three takes per line, one second of silence before and after each line, speak every line at the volume of the whole video. Send WAV or M4A; name files by the line id (O1, C02...). Keep all files from one session in one quality.

## What happens to the recording
1. The files go to the owner's folder, never to the repository without the performer's written consent for AI-assisted use (see below).
2. Lip-sync test (about $1 each, after the recording exists): Seedance 2.0 r2v with `audio_urls` and Kling 4.0 r2v with `voices`; the better one is used for the talking clips. If neither matches the lips well, the dialogue shots are cut to stay on the face only when the mouth is covered or turned, with the voice over them.
3. Disclosure: the video is marked AI-generated visuals; the voice is credited as "voice: a human performer" (only true if the human audio is the one heard; if a model re-speaks it, the voice is AI-cloned and is disclosed as such).
4. Performer rights: if it is a hired actor, a written licence for the voice used in this series (all videos, all platforms, AI-processing allowed) BEFORE the first publication.
