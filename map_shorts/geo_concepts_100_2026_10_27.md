## Upgrade plan for OUR videos (apply to every geo Short)
1. Two lengths only: LOOP 6-10 s (one map, one line, seamless loop) and STORY 20-35 s (one payoff). Drop 40-60 s unless it is a route.
2. Frame 0 = the map already moving + a 4-7 word claim/question on screen (no dark globe, no logo).
3. The payoff is done by the map: counter, drag, fill, split, route. Narration only supports it.
4. Participation line in every video: "Find your country 👇", "Which slice are you in?", ending with a binary choice (⬆️/⬇️, 1/2/3).
5. Series template in title + corner badge: "Can You Walk From X to Y? #4". Same opening sting and colour per series.
6. Titles = one concrete image or question, 1-2 emojis, no hashtag clutter in the title; hashtags in the description.
7. Weird Borders: show the visual line (floor, street, building) - own graphics on Wikimedia/CC photos with credits, or AI clearly labelled.
8. News slot: one map explainer within 48 h of a geography news event (flood, eruption, border change), facts from 2 sources.
9. Music: own energetic beat with cuts on the beat (competitors use trending songs; we cannot), sting on the payoff.
10. End frame = first frame (loop) for both formats.
11. Measure per video: swipe-away %, average % viewed, comments/1k, subscribers/1k; kill a template after 3 episodes under our median.
