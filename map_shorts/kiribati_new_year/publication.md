## Description
Kiribati straddled the International Date Line, so its eastern islands were almost a full day behind the west, and the two halves could do routine business only four days a week. In 1994 the country moved the date line around all its islands: the eastern half went from Friday, 30 December 1994 straight to Sunday, 1 January 1995, so Saturday, 31 December (New Year's Eve) never happened there. Its eastern islands are now the first in the world to greet each new year (UTC+14). 🎆🌏

Numbers (approximate, from Wikipedia): independence 12 July 1979; Gilbert Islands UTC+12, Phoenix and Line Islands were UTC-11/-10 before the change and UTC+13/+14 after; the date line moved about 1,000 km east; the Line Islands (including Kiritimati) started the year 2000 before any other country.
Sources: Wikipedia "Time in Kiribati", "Kiribati", "International Date Line".
