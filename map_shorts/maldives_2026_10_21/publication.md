## Description
The Maldives has an average ground elevation of about 1.5 m above sea level, which is commonly cited as the lowest of any country.
Malé, the capital, covers one island, and Velana Airport has an island of its own.

Visuals: satellite imagery (Esri World Imagery, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics), borders and coastlines (Natural Earth), animation drawn by us.
Music and sound effects are synthesized. No voice.
Facts: average elevation about 1.5 m (CIA World Factbook); about 1,190 islands in 26 atolls; global sea level is rising (IPCC).
