Africa has lions, zebras and giraffes. Madagascar is just four hundred kilometers away. So why is there not one lion?
Madagascar broke away about one hundred eighty million years ago. Lions appeared only two million years ago.
So its animals evolved alone. Over ninety percent of its wildlife exists nowhere else.
Lemurs floated there on rafts of vegetation, sixty million years ago.
The top predator? The fossa. It looks like a cat, but isn't.
