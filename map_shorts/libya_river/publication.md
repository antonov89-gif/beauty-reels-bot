## Description
Why did Libya build a giant river under the desert? 🏜️ More than 90% of Libya is desert and it has no permanent rivers, so almost everyone lives on the coast, where fresh water was running out. In the 1950s, while searching for oil deep in the Sahara, engineers found a huge reserve of water in the Nubian Sandstone Aquifer, stored thousands of years ago when the Sahara was green. In 1984 Libya started the Great Man-Made River: more than 1,300 wells, pipes 4 metres wide and over 2,800 km of pipeline. Today it supplies around 70% of Libya's fresh water. The catch: it is fossil water that barely refills.
