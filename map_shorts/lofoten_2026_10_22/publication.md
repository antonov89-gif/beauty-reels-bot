## Description
Reine, a fishing village in the Lofoten islands of Norway, lies north of the Arctic Circle (about 68 degrees north). Yet the sea around Lofoten usually stays ice-free in winter, warmed by the North Atlantic Current (the northern branch of the Gulf Stream); this is a general description, not a measured statistic.
