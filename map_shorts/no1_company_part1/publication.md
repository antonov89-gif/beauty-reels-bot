## Fact notes
- "Number one company" = the most representative company, as in the reference — not ranked by revenue or market cap.
- Nvidia: "at times the most valuable company in the world" (it has held #1 by market cap at several points since 2024).
- TSMC: makes most of the world's most advanced chips. ASML: sole supplier of EUV lithography machines.
- Turkish Airlines: flies to more countries than any other airline. Ethiopian: largest network in Africa.
- Morocco/OCP: Morocco holds roughly 70% of world phosphate reserves (USGS).
- Ferrari phrasing kept vague ("deliberately limited numbers"); deliveries are ~13–14k cars/year.
