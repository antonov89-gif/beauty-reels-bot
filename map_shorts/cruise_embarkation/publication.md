## Fact notes (hedged in the script)
- Check-in windows: assigned times are standard on major lines.
- Boarding closes typically 60-90 min before departure ("an hour or more").
- Closed-loop US cruises: US citizens may sail with birth certificate + government photo ID; passport recommended.
- Muster: required by SOLAS; most lines require it before sailing; many use digital eMuster ("many lines").
- Drink packages: some lines (e.g. Royal Caribbean, Celebrity) require all adults in the cabin to buy ("on some cruise lines").
- Automatic gratuities: per person per day on most lines; prepay possible on many.
- Maritime cellular networks charge high roaming rates.
