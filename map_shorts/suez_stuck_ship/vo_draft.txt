In 2021, a ship four hundred meters long got stuck in the Suez Canal, and twelve percent of world trade stopped.
Tugboats pulled. Diggers dug. Pumps emptied the ship. Nothing worked, and every hour cost four hundred million dollars.
Six days later, help came from the sky: a supermoon, and an extra-high tide.
Fourteen tugboats pulled together, and the ship broke free.
Over four hundred ships had been waiting.
The owner paid five hundred forty million dollars, and threw in a tugboat.
