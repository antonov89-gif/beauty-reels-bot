# GTA 6 ep.2 "Guess the REAL Vice City spot": prompts (frames + animation)

Rule for the quiz: every frame must show the real clue of the place (shape of the hotels, the causeway, the round white arena roof, the little elevated train, the murals, the chain of islands). No readable text, no real logos, no Rockstar characters.

## STYLE LOCK (put before every frame prompt)

Generic anonymous characters only, faces small or turned away. No logos, no readable text, no brand names, no HUD, no watermark.

Optional stronger tag (some generators block it, remove if refused): "in the visual style of the Grand Theft Auto VI trailers".
