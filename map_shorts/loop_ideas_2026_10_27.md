# 10 LOOP ideas for 100k+ (27.10.2026)
Why a new format: the owner's verdict on 27.10 was that narrated 35–45 s explainers keep landing at ~1.2–1.5k views and the goal is 100k+. Archetype B from geo_deep_analysis_2026_10_27.md (one-image loops at 6–23 s) reached 1.7–30M views on channels with 4k–39k subscribers.

## Format rules (every loop)
1. 6–9 s. The paradox is visible in frame 0: the map is already moving and a 4–7 word claim is on screen. It reads without sound.
2. One move only: overlay, drag, fill or counter. No narration.
3. Last frame = first frame (seamless loop) -> average viewed > 100%.
4. Sound: the owner adds a trending track from the YouTube/TikTok in-app library at upload (licensed for Shorts). The file carries our own beat as a fallback; no third-party audio is burned in.
5. Topics aimed at big Shorts audiences: India, Indonesia, Philippines, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Brazil, USA. The comment trigger is pride or disbelief ("no way", "my country").
6. YPP safety: one unique researched fact per loop, own graphics, NASA GIBS + Natural Earth (Esri-free), sources in the description. No meme characters, no fake claims, and the claim wording must match the fact (e.g. "lower 48").
7. Volume and kill rules: 3–4 loops/day, 2 hook variants per idea. Read at 48 h: engaged < 60% -> drop the template; any loop at 3x the median -> Part 2 within 24 h.
Honest outlook: 100k is a target per week, not a promise; no base rate is known.
