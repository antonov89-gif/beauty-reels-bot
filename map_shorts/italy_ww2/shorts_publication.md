## Description
Hitler's biggest problem in World War II wasn't the Soviets or America — it was Italy.
In 1940, Italy refused to join the attack on France, then rushed 300,000 men across the Alps once France was collapsing — and got stopped cold.
In Egypt, the 10th Army advanced about 100 km and dug in, until around 36,000 British troops chased it 800 km west and took over 100,000 prisoners.
Then Mussolini invaded Greece — and the Greeks pushed his army back into Albania, forcing Hitler to step in.
Some historians argue this delayed the invasion of the Soviet Union, leaving German troops to meet winter at the gates of Moscow.
