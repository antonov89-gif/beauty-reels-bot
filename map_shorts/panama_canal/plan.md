## 1. Concept and hooks

**Concept.** One ship, one day, one 82 km shortcut through a continent. It mixes money (tolls, $5.7 B revenue, $4 M auctions, 36 cents for a swimmer) with geography (oceans, mountains, a lake that is the canal's fuel tank, the Pacific entrance lying east of the Atlantic one). The video ends on the thesis: "the real currency of the canal is rain".

**Hooks, pick one:**
1. **(chosen)** "Every day, ships longer than three football fields line up to pay close to a million dollars for a shortcut just 82 kilometers long…"
2. "This August, a ship offered almost four million dollars just to skip a line of water."
3. "The most expensive 82 kilometers on Earth run on rain."

**Title options:**
- A Ship Pays $1,000,000 for 82 km — Inside the Panama Canal
- Why Ships Pay Millions to Cross 82 km of Water
- $4 Million to Skip the Line: How the Panama Canal Really Works
