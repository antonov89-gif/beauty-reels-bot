## s2 — Tequesta / Miami Circle (46.4 s) `short_s2.mp4`
Title: GTA 6 Hid a 2,000-Year-Old Secret in Vice City 🤯 #gta6 #vicecity #history
Description:
A road sign in an official GTA 6 screenshot points to Tequesta. Fans match it to Brickell, Miami. The Tequesta were the people at the mouth of the Miami River, and in 1998 archaeologists found the Miami Circle there: 38 ft across, 24 holes, possibly up to 2,000 years old (the age is debated), now a National Historic Landmark.
