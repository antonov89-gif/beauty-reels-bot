How we sorted it
- OFFICIAL TEXT: named in Rockstar's own descriptions (Ocean Beach, VC Port, Tisha-Wocka, Little Cuba).
- OFFICIAL SIGN: read on signs in official trailers or screenshots (Tequesta, Peacock Bay, Stockyard).
- OFFICIAL REGION: Leonida Keys, Grassrivers.
- FAN MATCH: links spotted by fans and matched to a real map. Rockstar has never published a list of real places.

Real facts in this video: Miami Beach Architectural District (about 800 buildings, National Register 14 May 1979), PortMiami ("Cruise Capital of the World"), MacArthur Causeway (1920), Miami Circle (found 1998, 38 ft, 24 holes, National Historic Landmark), Peacock Park and the Bay View House (about 1882), Opa-locka (1926, Glenn Curtiss), Little Havana and Domino Park, Wynwood Walls (2009), Kaseya Center (1999), Metromover (1986, free since 2002), Venetian Causeway (12 bridges), Overseas Highway (about 113 miles, 42 bridges), Everglades National Park (1947), Miami incorporated 28 July 1896.
