## Fact notes
- Pope puffer-jacket image: viral in March 2023, AI-generated (not shown in the video).
- ChatGPT: launched Nov 2022; reported to pass 100 million users in about two months (UBS estimate, Jan 2023).
- Wright brothers: first flight Dec 17, 1903, 12 seconds. Apollo 11 landing: July 20, 1969 (66 years).
- Deep Blue beat Kasparov in 1997. AlphaGo beat Lee Sedol in 2016.
- Music: Warner settled with Suno and Udio, Universal settled with Udio (Oct-Nov 2025), moving to licensed models.
- METR: measured that the length of tasks AI can complete is doubling every few months. The bar chart is illustrative.
- Stanford (Brynjolfsson et al.): early-career workers in the most AI-exposed jobs saw weaker employment; driven mostly by less hiring. The chart is illustrative of direction, not exact figures.
- AGI survey: 2023 survey of AI researchers (AI Impacts), median around 2047; forecasts have since moved earlier.
- EU AI Act entered into force Aug 2024, applying in stages.
