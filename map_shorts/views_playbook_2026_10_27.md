# Views playbook v1: what a 100k+ Short needs (27.10.2026)
Sources: our channel data (vidIQ 27.09–27.10), market sweeps (vidIQ outliers, Nexlev), 3 reference analyses, the Stonehenge and Iraq productions today, geo_*_2026_10_27.md, strategy_summary_2026_10_27.md. Facts about the audience are OBSERVED data; the formula below is a HYPOTHESIS until the Iraq result (48 h) and the next 3 episodes confirm it.

## 1. Where we are (OBSERVED)
- 50 subscribers; 45.9k views in 30 d over 41 videos; median 1,204; 90 % of views come from the Shorts feed; engaged 49 % (half swipe away); likes 1.99 %; 1.22 subscribers per 1k views.
- YPP (YouTube Help, 27.10): 1,000 subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 d OR 4,000 long-form watch hours in 12 mo. At ~1.5k views a day we are 74x short. Linear growth cannot close it; we need hits (1M+ each, several per 90 d).
- Our weak formats (owner's verdict and data): narrated 35–45 s explainers; engine-only loops on a dark globe (three made today, judged weaker); one niche fact (an exclave, a town).

## 2. What the winners do (OBSERVED in vidIQ, this month, channels < 100k subscribers)

They win on: one instantly readable paradox in frame 0, many short beats, visible stakes, a binary question, and a series template. They do not win on cut speed alone (2.5–6 events / 10 s on the biggest stories) or on facts alone.

## 3. The formula for our next videos (HYPOTHESIS, v1)
1. Frame 0: the map already moving, a 3–5 word claim in a banner, a number ("ONE 58 KM DOOR?!").
2. Escalation, not a list: giant vs tiny number -> villain neighbour -> "Wait, but…" objection -> hidden stake (oil, water, money) -> the legal or physical mechanism -> punch image.
3. A new meaning every 3–4 s: a chip with a number, a stamp slam, a 3D prop, a counter, a measure line.
4. Ending: a binary question, never an outro. Pinned comment repeats it. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
5. Length: 45–55 s for a conflict story (each beat is a new reason to stay); 6–12 s only for flag/compare lists.
6. Topics: whole countries, famous places, money or power stakes. Audiences with big Shorts volume (US, IN, PH, GB, ID).
7. Series: same look, same sting, same question format; kill a template after 3 episodes under our median; Part 2 within 24 h of any 3x outlier.

## 4. Production standard (what "premium" means for us, learned today)
- Accurate base: Esri-free imagery (NASA Blue Marble, Copernicus Sentinel-2) + Natural Earth borders drawn with our own code, so geography is true.
- 3D restyle: Nano Banana edit (about $0.02) turns the flat layout into a tilted cinematic 3D map with relief, water and light. Props are added by the model; text is never generated by AI.
- Motion: Veo 3.1 fast image-to-video (about $0.18 / 8 s). Always check the contact sheet: AI drifts (a made-up line, layers vanishing, a country changing colour). Cut before the drift, slow down, or continue with a push on the last clean frame; regenerate only when needed.
- Our graphics on top: banner, rotated stamps, chips, 2–4 word captions with the active word yellow, number counters. Own synth beat + SFX on every event (6–10 per 10 s).
- Gates before showing: STT of the voice, loudness −14 LUFS, bed audible in gaps, last-frame check, authenticity audit, AI flags ON, sources in the description.
- Cost per Short: ~$2 + ~2 RUB voice. Time: storyboard -> owner OK -> pilot of 3 clips -> batch.

## 5. Mistakes to avoid (all made in the last days)
Explainers with a single niche fact; engine-only dark globes; three lines of title text; claims stronger than the source (we fixed "Indonesia wider than the USA" with "without Alaska & Hawaii"); invented distances ("2 km" between ports was wrong, 13 km); linking oil exports to a channel they do not use; a human mascot for real nations; paid generation without a pilot.

## 6. KPIs per video (read at 48 h)
Views vs median 1.2k (target 3x, then 100k), engaged views % (target ≥ 60), average % viewed, likes % (≥ 2), comments per 1k (≥ 0.5), subscribers per 1k (≥ 2). Weekly goal: one video above 100k and engaged ≥ 65 %.
