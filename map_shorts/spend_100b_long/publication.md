## Description
You wake up with $100 billion. The only rule: spend all of it. Supercars, mega mansions, private jets, a superyacht, three sports teams, the most expensive painting ever sold, the Las Vegas Sphere, a cruise ship, an air force and an aircraft carrier... and you still haven't spent half. Then the real problem starts: the money keeps growing faster than you can spend it.
