## Description
Why did one of the biggest lakes on Earth vanish in a single human lifetime? 🌊 In 1960 the Aral Sea in Central Asia was the fourth largest lake in the world. Then Soviet irrigation canals pulled water from the two rivers that fed it, to grow cotton in the desert. The sea shrank to roughly a tenth of its original area, ports ended up far from the water, and the dry seabed turned into a source of salty, toxic dust. In 2005 Kazakhstan finished the Kok-Aral dam to save the northern part, and fish came back there, but the southern part is still shrinking.
