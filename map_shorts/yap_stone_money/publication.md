## Description
On Yap, a small island in Micronesia 🇫🇲, the traditional money is giant stone wheels called rai. Some are tiny, but the biggest is over three metres wide and weighs about four tons 🪨

There is no such stone on Yap. Sailors brought it from Palau 🇵🇼, over 400 km away, by canoe and bamboo raft, and many stones sank on the way 🌊 The harder the trip, the more the stone was worth. Big stones don't move when they change hands: everyone simply remembers who owns them. One stone is said to lie at the bottom of the sea, and people kept trading it anyway 🤝

Today Yap uses US dollars at the shop 💵, but stone money is still used for weddings and land 💍

Numbers are approximate. Sources: Wikipedia "Rai stones", "Yap".
