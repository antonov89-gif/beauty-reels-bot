## Description
In 1892 American traders persuaded Samoa's king to switch to the American date, so Monday, 4 July 1892 happened twice. In 2011 Samoa jumped back across the International Date Line to line up with New Zealand, Australia and Asia, and Friday, 30 December 2011 never happened there.

Sources: Wikipedia "International Date Line" (1892: Monday 4 July repeated; 2011: Friday 30 December removed), "Time in Samoa" (Thu 29 -> Sat 31 Dec 2011; PM: to improve business with New Zealand, Australia, China and Asia). Samoa is UTC+13, American Samoa UTC-11.
