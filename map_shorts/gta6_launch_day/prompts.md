# GTA 6 ep.3 "LAUNCH DAY": prompts (cinematic v2)

Concept: what happens to the world when GTA 6 launches (19 Nov 2026, midnight). Cold open -> launch wave over a real night-lights map -> store lines + the 90M-views trailer record -> money (GTA V benchmark) -> "blast zone" calendar -> servers and streamers -> the game world -> 200M copies -> "where will you be at midnight?".

## STYLE LOCK v2 (before every frame prompt)
Cinematic trailer frame for a next-gen open-world crime game set in a fictional sunny Vice City (Miami, Florida): shot on 35mm anamorphic lenses, cinematic 2.39:1 composition adapted to vertical 9:16 with strong foreground, midground and background depth layers, hyper-detailed real-time game render look (NOT a photograph), motivated practical lighting, volumetric haze and light shafts, rim light on characters, teal-and-orange grade with hot pink and cyan neon accents, anamorphic lens flares, light film grain, shallow depth of field, rich environmental storytelling, generic anonymous characters with faces turned away or small. No logos, no readable text, no brand names, no HUD, no watermark.

## Frames (9:16, best quality)
- K1 (hook): Epic night aerial: the camera emerges from low storm clouds high above a glowing Vice City-style coastline, a vast skyline of glass towers with pink and cyan neon, a long causeway of car headlights across black water, fireworks bursting over the bay, a thin searchlight sweeping the clouds.
- K2 (the lines): Midnight outside a game store on a neon-lit Miami-style street after rain: a huge crowd of excited anonymous fans queueing around the block, phones raised, cheering, some in sunglasses and Hawaiian shirts, wet asphalt reflecting pink neon, a delivery scooter and a police cruiser at the curb, glowing shop windows, low angle.
- K3 (watching the trailer): A teenager seen from behind sitting very close to a big TV in a dark bedroom, the screen lighting the room in warm sunset orange and pink, posters and sneakers on the floor, controller in hands, energy drink cans, over-the-shoulder composition, cinematic rim light.
- K5 (the money): Golden-hour rooftop in a Vice City-style city: a black leather briefcase packed with neatly stacked cash opens in the foreground, bills lifting in the wind, a glowing skyline and a helicopter behind, long cinematic shadows, glossy loafers and a suit sleeve in frame, no faces.
- K7 (servers): A vast futuristic data center corridor, endless racks of servers with pulsing pink and cyan lights, thin fog, polished reflective floor, a lone technician silhouette far away walking toward the camera, cold light shafts, symmetric one-point perspective.
- K8 (streamers): A professional streamer room at night: a gamer in a headset, face turned away, in front of three glowing monitors showing blurred neon beach scenes, RGB lights, chat shown only as abstract colored bars (no readable text), a ring light, a wall of palm-tree posters, haze and rim light.
- K10 (ending): A hand about to press the power button of a sleek black generic game console (no logos) on a TV stand in a dark living room, the TV glowing with a sunset-pink reflection on the hand, a wall clock showing midnight (hands only, no numbers), macro shallow depth of field.

## Animation (6 s, ONE main move each; append: " Cinematic trailer camera, smooth realistic motion, no cuts, no text, no logos.")
- K1: The camera dives forward through the clouds toward the glowing skyline, fireworks burst over the bay, the headlights stream along the causeway, a searchlight sweeps across the sky. Fast cinematic drone dive.
- K2: Slow dolly push-in toward the crowd, fans cheer and raise their phones, neon signs flicker, a few people step forward as the doors begin to open, wet street reflections shimmer.
- K3: Slow push-in over the shoulder, the TV light pulses warm orange and pink, the teenager leans closer, the controller trembles slightly with excitement, dust floats in the light.
- K5: The briefcase lid pops fully open, bills flutter and lift in the wind, the camera pushes in slowly while the helicopter crosses the skyline, golden light flares across the lens.
- K7: Smooth forward glide down the corridor, the rack lights pulse faster and faster in a wave toward the camera, the fog drifts, the technician keeps walking.
- K8: Slow orbit around the streamer, the monitors flicker with neon beach scenes, the colored chat bars scroll faster, the ring light flashes, the streamer raises both hands in excitement.
- K10: The finger presses the power button, a soft white light turns on, the TV glows brighter and floods the room with golden sunset color, camera slowly pushes in.
